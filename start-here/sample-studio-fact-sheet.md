This is what a finished fact sheet looks like. The studio is made up. Yours can be shorter. Short true answers beat long polished ones, because the assistant quotes this back when it scores whether a client fits you.

## Studio basics

- Studio name: Hartwell Studio
- Positioning in one sentence: The packaging studio food and drink founders call when a range has outgrown its first design.
- Website and portfolio location: hartwellstudio.example, portfolio section
- Locations and geographic reach: Chicago. Work across the US, calls in US time zones.
- Team size and current capacity notes: 9 people. Could start one new project in the next 60 days, not two.

## Services

- Services offered: Packaging design (primary), brand identity for food and drink brands, range extension and adaptation, production-ready artwork through our pre-press partner.
- Services explicitly NOT offered: Web design, social media management, photography direction on location, media buying.
- Deliverables: Design in-house. Print production managed through a named partner, quoted as a pass-through with a stated markup.
