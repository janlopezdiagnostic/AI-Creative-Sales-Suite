# Example 2: DTC Brand Refresh (Medium)

All brands in this example are fictional. The facts below are invented for training.

**Scenario:** A brand and packaging studio asks for a brief on Loom & Field (fictional), a direct-to-consumer home fragrance brand (candles and diffusers) that sells online and through two small retail partners.

## Raw input (as received, messy)

> "Loom & Field, the candle people. Their site looks tired, honestly the whole brand looks 2021. Someone said they're 'exploring retail'. Our studio did the identity and packaging for a home goods DTC brand two years ago and a candle brand's packaging refresh (no identity). Worth a look?"

Plus pasted material: the brand's own blog post dated 2026-06-02 (fictional) announcing a "new chapter" and an expanded product line including room sprays; a screenshot of the current site, unchanged in structure since 2022; no trade coverage found; no leadership news found; no funding news found.

## Reasoning

- Intake: origin is the seller's own observation. No relationship. Conflict check: clear.
- Sweep: only one real signal. The June blog post confirms a line expansion into room sprays (line extension, confirmed from a primary source, dated 2026-06-02). But it is now October: the signal is over four months old, and the sprays are already on sale on the site, in packaging that visibly extends the existing system. The design window for that launch has closed.
- "Exploring retail" cannot be sourced. No trade article, no announcement, no job post. It is graded UNVERIFIED and cannot carry a brief.
- Refresh cycle: the identity does look dated, and packaging is from 2021 to 2022. Per the playbook, age alone earns a watchlist entry, not outreach. Age plus the closed extension signal is still not a stack pointing at open work.
- Trees: Tree 1 WEAK to MODERATE (one confirmed but closed signal, one unverified signal): exit is WATCHLIST. Tree 2 portfolio match 2: same deliverables (identity, packaging refresh) in an adjacent category, partial proof. Tree 3 timing: UNKNOWN for any refresh; the extension window is CLOSED. Tree 4 buyer UNKNOWN: no named brand lead found anywhere; the founder appears to run brand personally, but that is an inference from social posts, so it is recorded as probable founder-led, unverified.
- Honest summary: this is a brand that plausibly refreshes within a year and a studio that could do the work. Plausible is not a trigger.

## Final brief (condensed)

**Recommendation: WATCHLIST.** Review triggers: first physical retail placement announcement, any new product category launch, a brand or creative leadership hire, or 90 days, whichever comes first.

**Hypothesis (held, not actioned):** If Loom & Field (fictional) moves into physical retail at scale, its screen-first packaging, designed in 2021, will likely need a shelf-impact review, and the studio's DTC home goods proof is relevant. Confidence: LOW. The verification step that would raise it: a sourced retail placement or a dated refresh announcement.

| Fit | Score | Basis |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Portfolio match | 2 | Identity and packaging proof, adjacent categories |
| Deal size fit | 2 | Unknown; DTC refresh plausibly in range (estimate) |
| Timing | 1 | No open window identified |

## Critique

The trap in this input was the seller's enthusiasm ("the whole brand looks 2021") and an unsourced retail rumour. A weak brief would have converted both into a refresh hypothesis with medium confidence. The correct moves were grading the rumour UNVERIFIED, noticing the one confirmed signal had already closed, and letting Tree 3 cap the outcome. The watchlist triggers are specific, so the account gets rechecked at the moment it becomes real instead of being pitched early and burned.
