# Example 3: Fashion Label, Seasonal Cycle (Medium)

All brands in this example are fictional. The facts below are invented for training.

**Scenario:** A design studio with fashion clients asks for a brief on Marisol Atelier (fictional), an independent womenswear label selling through its own stores, e-commerce, and wholesale accounts.

## Raw input (as received, messy)

> "Marisol Atelier - they just showed SS27 at fashion week, got decent coverage. Their lookbooks and campaign stuff is done by a freelancer I think, quality is hit and miss. Wholesale is growing, they mentioned department stores in an interview. We've done lookbooks + campaign for two labels, and packaging for their shopping bags/boxes for one. Seasonal work, obviously."

Plus pasted material: a trade interview dated 2026-09-18 (fictional) in which the founder says wholesale orders grew and two department store accounts start with the AW27 season; fashion week coverage dated 2026-09-30 (fictional) showing the SS27 collection; the label's site showing AW26 campaign imagery credited to a freelance photographer, with no studio credit anywhere.

## Reasoning

- Intake: origin is the seller's market knowledge. No relationship. Conflict check: clear, the studio's two fashion clients are in different price tiers and do not list Marisol as a competitor.
- Sweep: signals are real but cyclical. Wholesale expansion into department stores for AW27 is confirmed (founder interview, trade press, 2026-09-18). Wholesale growth creates concrete design needs: line sheets, lookbooks that sell to buyers, packaging for retail presentation, campaign assets cut for partner use. Fashion week coverage confirms the label is active and investing in presentation.
- Timing is the whole game here. SS27 has already shown; its campaign work is done. AW27 selling happens in showrooms months before the season: wholesale materials are briefed well ahead of the selling window, which for AW27 opens early next year. So the open window is AW27 wholesale materials and campaign, opening this coming quarter. Research done in the wrong month would have called this either closed (SS27) or vague (no season named).
- No incumbent studio is named anywhere. Campaign credit sits with a freelancer, which suggests project-by-project buying rather than a retained studio. Graded as reported inference, stated as such.
- Trees: Tree 1 MODERATE (confirmed signals, but the work is recurring seasonal rather than a single transformation). Tree 2 portfolio match 3 for lookbooks and campaign, 2 for retail packaging: scored 3 overall with the packaging gap noted. Tree 3 timing THIS QUARTER / NEXT: the SS27 cycle is closed, but the relevant window is not that cycle. The AW27 wholesale window has a dated opening next quarter (two department store accounts starting with AW27, with materials briefed ahead of the selling window), so this is Tree 3's dated-future-trigger branch, not its SEASONAL branch; SEASONAL is for a recurring cycle with no dated opening in view. The THIS QUARTER / NEXT exit is watchlist with a review date before the window opens, or pursue now with a relationship-opening angle if portfolio match is 3. Match is 3, so the brief takes the pursue-now branch, in that limited form only. Tree 4 buyer IDENTIFIED (FOUNDER): the founder is the public face and decision maker in all sources; no brand director is named.

## Final brief (condensed)

**Recommendation: PURSUE NOW (relationship-opening angle only).** Next step: a short, specific introduction referencing the AW27 wholesale expansion and the studio's lookbook work for comparable labels. Not a pitch for SS27 (closed) and not a generic capabilities deck. Review at the start of the AW27 selling window.

**Hypothesis:** Because Marisol Atelier (fictional) adds two department store accounts for AW27, it will need wholesale selling materials and a campaign built to work in partner channels, briefed ahead of the AW27 selling window. The studio can credibly win it because it has shipped lookbooks and campaigns for two labels at an adjacent tier. Confidence: MEDIUM (window and proof are solid; whether the work leaves the current freelance setup is unknown).

| Fit | Score | Basis |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Portfolio match | 3 | Lookbook and campaign proof in category; packaging gap noted |
| Deal size fit | 2 | Seasonal projects, lower end of range (estimate) |
| Timing | 2 | Next cycle opens within 1 to 2 quarters |

## Critique

Fashion research fails when it ignores the calendar. The same facts read in a different month produce a different correct answer, and this brief names its season explicitly in every section so a reader can spot when it expires. The weakness to flag: deal size on seasonal fashion work is genuinely uncertain, and the brief keeps it at 2 rather than flattering the total. If the studio's floor is strict, this account flips to watchlist on deal size alone, and the brief's scoring makes that one-line change easy to see.
