# Weekly Watchlist Review

**Week of:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Reviewed by:** [Name]
**Studio:** [Studio name, from fact sheet]

One row per watchlist account. The watchlist is where briefs that were not yet pursue-now wait for their trigger, and this sheet is the weekly discipline that keeps them alive: check each account against the trigger named in its brief, record what moved, set the next check, and issue a verdict. An account that is never reviewed is not on a watchlist; it is in a drawer.

## Field guide

- **Signal:** The specific trigger the brief named as the review event: a retailer reset, a leadership appointment, a launch, a range submission deadline. Not "the account" in general. If the brief named no trigger, that is the finding this week: set one or close the account.
- **Date:** The date of the signal itself, not the date of this review. Dates are what separate a live watchlist from a pile of old enthusiasm; a signal older than six months with nothing newer is Stale and gets treated accordingly.
- **Movement since last check:** Use one word from the list, then one short clause of evidence with its source and date.
  - **New:** a signal appeared that was not in the brief.
  - **Strengthened:** a second, independent signal now points at the same work in the same window (a stack forming).
  - **Unchanged:** checked against the trigger, nothing new found. Record what was checked, not just the verdict; "checked trade press and the brand's news page, nothing since [date]" is a real entry.
  - **Weakened:** the original signal lost support, for example a launch slipping or a pledge going quiet.
  - **Stale:** the signal aged past six months with no reactivation.
  - **Fired:** the trigger event happened. This row leaves the watchlist and becomes a fresh brief, scored from the top.
- **Next check date:** A date or a named event, whichever the trigger dictates: the reset date, the appointment announcement, the next seasonal cycle, or a maximum of 90 days out. Never leave this blank; a row with no next check date is closed by default at the following review.
- **Verdict:** One of the module's outcomes. PURSUE means the account graduates to a pursue-now brief and routes to the outreach skill, not that outreach happens from this sheet. WATCHLIST means it stays, with the movement and next check recorded above. DO NOT PURSUE means it leaves the watchlist and is filed with the reasoning, so it is not re-researched from scratch next quarter; do-not-pursue accounts get one light re-check two quarters out per the playbook cadence.

## Review rules

- **Every row gets touched.** Skipping an account silently is how windows get missed. If there was no time to check it, its movement is "not checked" and the next check date moves up, not out.
- **Every claim of movement needs a source and a date**, the same rule as the briefs themselves. A movement recorded from memory is [UNVERIFIED] until a source is attached.
- **Graduate or kill, do not accumulate.** Accounts should leave this sheet in one direction or the other. A watchlist that only grows is a hiding place for decisions nobody wants to make.
- **Feed the signal log.** New and Strengthened movements get a row in `templates/signal-log-template.md` the same week, so the next brief on that account starts from the log, not from this sheet alone.
