## Field guide

- **Signal type:** Use the playbook taxonomy: Rebrand, Leadership, Line extension, SKU proliferation, Funding / M&A, Retailer reset, Sustainability / material, Refresh cycle, Packaging structure.
- **Grade:** Confirmed (primary source or two independent sources), Reported (one credible source), UNVERIFIED (weak or single social source, or inference), Stale (was real, now older than 6 months with no reactivation).
- **Status:** In brief, Watchlist, Rejected, Converted (became outreach), Closed (window passed).
- **Review date:** The date or event that should reopen this row. Tie it to a trigger (reset date, launch date, appointment announcement), not just a calendar guess.
