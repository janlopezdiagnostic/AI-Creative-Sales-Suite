# Walk-Away Email Template

Use only after decision tree 5 has run and the founder's decision is on the record. Send once. Do not send a follow-up paragraph, a revised offer, or a "just checking" note afterwards; silence after this email is part of the message.

---

Subject: [Project name]: a straight answer

[Name],

Thank you for [the specific real thing: considering us for the range redesign / the time your team gave the proposal / the honest conversation about the numbers]. I have taken a proper look at whether we can make the structure work, and I owe you a straight answer rather than a slow one.

We cannot [do the work at the reduced rate on the full scope / work inside the terms as drafted / sign the exclusivity in that form, pick the true one]. [One honest sentence of reason, structural not personal: "Our price is what this scope costs done properly, and a version at that number would short-change the work itself." / "Those terms ask a studio our size to carry financing and risk we are not built to carry."]

That is a fit problem, not a quality judgement in either direction. If [the condition that would genuinely change the answer: a smaller first phase ever fits the budget / a future project runs on standard terms / the category position changes], I would be glad to pick the conversation back up, and the proposal structure we built would still be a good starting point.

Wishing you and the team well with [the launch / the range / the reset, name the real thing]. [Optional, only if true: "If it would help, I can suggest two studios whose model fits this structure better than ours."]

[Name]

---

Internal notes (delete before sending):
- The email contains no discount, no new structure, and no blame. If a draft of this email offers anything, it is not a walk-away email; return to decision tree 2.
- The condition in paragraph three must be real. A decorative door ("maybe one day") invites negotiation; a real condition ("a phase 1 at this smaller scope") is pipeline.
- Log the send, the trigger, and the review date (if the condition has a natural moment: budget cycle, reset, leadership change) in the negotiation log and CRM the same day.
- If they reply with movement, that is a new negotiation: re-run triage, escalate the decision, and do not let relief at being wanted set the terms.
