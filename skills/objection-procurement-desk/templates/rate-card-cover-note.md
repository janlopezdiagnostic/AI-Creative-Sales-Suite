# Rate Card Cover Note

Send this with every rate card submission: panel applications, procurement requests, framework negotiations. Attach it as the first page or the covering email. Do not send a naked rate card.

---

[Studio letterhead or email header]
Date: [date]  |  Rate card version: [version, matching the version on the card itself]

Dear [name / procurement team],

Our current rate card is attached, version [x], valid to [date: the review date stated on the card].

Three things about how to read it, so it is useful to you rather than misleading:

**1. The rates are costing units, not the product.** Our project work is priced on deliverables: a named scope, a fixed fee per phase, a defined number of revision rounds, and production support where listed. Day rates apply to time-based work (ongoing support, embedded days, discovery extensions) and to change orders. Comparing a project price to a single day rate compares a finished thing to one of its ingredients.

**2. What a rate carries.** Each rate includes the role's full cost to us: [TO CONFIRM before sending: list only what your rates actually carry, for example employment costs, insurance, software and font licensing, studio overhead] and the revision structure stated in our proposals. It is not comparable to a freelance day rate for the same title, which excludes most of that list and all of the accountability for the outcome.

**3. Role mix is the honest comparison.** Projects are staffed as a blend. On a typical packaging programme the blend runs [describe in general terms from the studio's own mix, for example "roughly a third senior design, a third design and adaptation, a third artwork, production and management"]. We are glad to walk through the staffing of any specific proposal.

If your process needs a benchmark record, this note plus the card is our submission, and we are happy to answer questions about it. If a specific project is being costed, we will price the deliverables and show how the price is built, phase by phase.

[Name, role, studio]
[Contact]

---

Internal notes (delete before sending):
- The card attached must be the current version from the rate and rules sheet, dated, with its validity period on it. Never retype rates into this note; the figures live on the card.
- Never include another client's rates, your margins, or the founder's floor in this note or its covering email.
- If procurement replies that the card alone will be scored, that is diagnostic data: log it and run decision tree 3.
