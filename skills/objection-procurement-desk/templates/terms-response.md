# Terms Response Template

Use when client or portal terms conflict with the studio rate and rules sheet. One clause per row; never negotiate "the terms" as a lump. This document is drafting aid, have counsel review before use, and before signature in all cases.

## Summary for the covering email (draft)
"Thank you for the documents. We can accept [list acceptable clauses by name]. On [clause names], our standard structure is [summary], which we have set out in the attached response with the reasoning in each case. The points we cannot accept as drafted are [red line clauses], because [one honest sentence each]. Everything else in your document is fine with us. Once we are aligned on these points we are ready to [sign / complete onboarding], and work starts on [purchase order / signed SOW] as set out there."

## Rules for this document
- Every clause in their document that touches money, rights, or risk appears in the table. Silence on a clause reads as acceptance and will be treated as such later.
- Red line rows are escalated to the founder before this response is sent, with the recommendation recorded in the negotiation log.
- Nothing in this response quotes, references, or benchmarks another client's terms, including "our other clients accept this". Our terms stand on their own reasoning.
- The final agreed set is confirmed in one document, in writing, before work starts.
