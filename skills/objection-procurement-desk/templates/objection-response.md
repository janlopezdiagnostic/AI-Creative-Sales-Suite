# Objection Response Template

Objection answered: [library name, for example "Too expensive" / "In-house with AI"]
Diagnosis: [Price / Process / Risk / Politics, internal note, delete before sending]
Channel: [email / message / call script]  |  Deal: [client, project]  |  Date: [date]

## Structure (draft in this order; not every section is used every time)

**1. Acknowledge (one or two sentences, no apology for the price)**
[Thank them for the directness or the detail. Name the constraint respectfully: "Understood, Finance has a ceiling and my job is to fit the work to reality, not to argue with it."]

**2. Isolate (the diagnostic question, asked once)**
[For price: "If the number worked, is everything else in the proposal agreed?" For capability: "Which parts were you picturing in-house?" Use the playbook entry for this objection. If the answer is unknown, this question IS the message; send it and wait.]

**3. Answer with structure (the substance)**
[The deliverables walk, the trade menu, the phased option, the terms structure: whatever the playbook prescribes for this objection and diagnosis. Every trade named with its price effect, taken from the original scope build. The rate does not move.]

**4. Protect the yes (make agreement easy)**
[Restate what is agreed already. Name the smallest next commitment: choose a tier, approve the structure, a 20 minute call on a stated day. Remove every other decision from this message.]

**5. Close (one line, no sting)**
["Tell me which structure Finance prefers and I will paper it the same day." Adapt to the situation. Never end on a discount, a justification paragraph, or a question you do not actually want answered.]

## Before sending (internal, delete this section from the sent copy)
- [ ] Diagnosis stated above and the response answers the diagnosis, not just the sentence
- [ ] Every number traces to the proposal as sent or the rate sheet
- [ ] Every concession offered is a trade, with its exchange named
- [ ] Inside the sender's authority per the rate sheet, or escalated and decided
- [ ] Nothing about any other client appears, in any form
- [ ] User has reviewed this exact text and confirmed the send
