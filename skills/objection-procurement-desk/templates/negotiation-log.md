# Negotiation Log

One log per deal, started the day the proposal is sent, closed the day the deal is signed or walked. This is the internal record; the CRM summary is copied from it, never the other way round.

## Rules
- Entries are made the same day as the exchange. A log reconstructed at invoice time is fiction with dates.
- Agreements are recorded in the client's words where possible, and each triggers a same-day confirmation email (drafted by the desk, sent by the owner).
- The floor, margins, and other clients' information appear in this log only as references to the rate sheet ("per rate sheet"), never as numbers that could travel if the log is forwarded. When in doubt, write less.
