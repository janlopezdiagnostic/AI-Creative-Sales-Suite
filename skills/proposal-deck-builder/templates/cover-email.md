# Template: Cover Email

The email that carries the proposal or deck. Short enough to be read on a phone, complete enough that the document is never forwarded naked. This template produces a draft for the user to send; this skill never sends it (Standing rule 1).

Rules before drafting:
- Price placement honoured: if Tree 3 put the price in the meeting, this email contains no figures. If the price is in the document, the email may name the recommended tier and its total exactly as the scope output states it, or may simply point to the investment section; the user's call, recorded.
- Name the canonical version. From v2 onward, state what changed and that this version supersedes the earlier one.
- One next step, one date. The email asks for exactly the thing the proposal's Section 10 asks for, nothing extra.

---

Subject: [Proposal: project name in the client's language] [Add "v[n], supersedes v[n-1]" where relevant]

[Name],

[Line 1: what this is and why now, in one sentence, referencing the conversation or discovery it follows.]

Attached: [document name, version, format]. [One sentence on what to look at first: for example, the approach on page 3 and the timeline built back from your March reset.] [Where price sits, per the placement decision: "The investment is set out in Section 8, with the tier we recommend marked." Or, meeting branch: "As agreed, we will walk through the investment in Thursday's meeting."]

[Optional single line: the one thing the sender most wants noticed: a date held, a gate that needs the client's named consolidator, a question only the recipient can answer.]

Next step: [the action, for example, a 30 minute call on [date options] to walk through it, or approval of the recommended tier by reply]. [What happens immediately after: deposit invoice the same day, kickoff on [date] held in our calendar.]

Thanks for the conversation that got us here. [Sign-off]
[Name, role, studio, phone]

Attachments: [canonical file names exactly as attached]
