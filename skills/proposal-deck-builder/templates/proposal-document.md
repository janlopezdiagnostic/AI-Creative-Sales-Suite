# Template: Proposal Document

Build the client-facing proposal from this skeleton. Bracketed notes are instructions to the drafter and are deleted before the document is final. Scope, price, and terms are transcribed from the Scope and Pricing Architect output, never rewritten. Anything legal-adjacent carries the flag: "Drafting aid, have counsel review before use."

---

[Studio name] · Proposal for [Client / brand]
[Project name in the client's language] · [Date] · Version [n] · Prepared for [named decision maker]
This version supersedes all earlier versions. [Include this line from v2 onward.]

## 1. Understanding
[Two to four paragraphs mirroring the discovery brief: the situation, the deadline pressure, the stakes, what success looks like to the client. Client's facts only, no studio claims, no invented figures.]

## 2. What we see
[The insight: one page maximum. The category, shelf, or architecture observation that reframes the problem, sourced from the audit, shelf evidence, or client-supplied data named in the brief. Observation only; nothing here designs the client's answer.]

## 3. Approach
[Phases in order. Per phase: what happens, what the client sees, the approval gate, what the studio needs from the client. Name the risks managed explicitly: SKU count, copy and regulatory timing, dieline changes, consolidated feedback.]

## 4. Relevant work
[Two to four cases, ordered by rhyme with this client's problem. Per case, use the case-study-page template: problem, what we did, deliverables, outcome where the library permits citation, imagery where it does not.]

## 5. Scope
[Transcribed tiers from the scope output. Per tier: deliverables with quantities (SKUs, rounds, formats), what changes between tiers. Exclusions in plain language, in this section, at body prominence. Assumptions listed. Any open item reads [TO CONFIRM].]

## 6. Team
[Named people, role on this project, one relevant past project each. See the team rules in the playbook, Part 5.]

## 7. Timeline
[Phases against the client's fixed points, built backwards from the deadline. Client obligations with owners and dates. Critical path and the most likely delay, with its mitigation.]

## 8. Investment
[Tier table from the scope output, figures exact. Recommended tier marked. What is not included, restated briefly with a pointer to Section 5. Payment shape: deposit, milestones, balance timing.]

## 9. Terms summary
[Plain-language summary of the commercial terms from the scope output: deposit and milestones, revision caps and extra round price, kill fee by stage, change order rule, source file and usage position. Labelled as a summary; the SOW governs.]
Drafting aid, have counsel review before use.

## 10. Next step
[One action, one owner, one date: for example, approve the Better tier by reply, deposit invoice issued the same day, kickoff held on [date]. State what happens immediately after. Nothing else goes in this section.]

## Assumptions
[Every assumption the document rests on: inputs taken as fixed, user instructions that overrode a default, open [TO CONFIRM] items and who resolves them.]

## Appendix (optional)
[Credentials one-pager content, fuller case detail, compliance or insurance facts for procurement. Nothing in the appendix may contradict or quietly extend the main sections.]
