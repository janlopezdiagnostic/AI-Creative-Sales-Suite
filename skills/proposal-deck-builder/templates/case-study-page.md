# Template: Case Study Page

One case per page in a deck, one case per block in a proposal document. The discipline is identical in both. Before drafting, read the library entry's permission note for this specific use: named or anonymised, outcome citable or not, imagery permitted or not. The permission governs every field below.

## The problem
[Two or three lines, from the library entry: the situation when the studio was hired, the deadline or pressure, what was at stake. No adjectives doing the work of facts.]

## What we did
[Three to five lines: the intervention, the phases, the judgement calls that mattered. This is where process credibility lives: name the architecture decision, the production constraint solved, the gate that caught a problem.]

## Delivered
[The deliverables as a countable list: SKUs, families, formats, guidelines, structural work, production support. Quantities match the library entry exactly.]

## Outcome

If permission does not exist, delete this section entirely and let the work stand on the sections above; do not replace it with adjectives.]
