# Template: Deck Outline

One row per page. Headline is a claim the page can substantiate, stated as a sentence. Visual names material from the case library or process evidence, never the client's product designed speculatively. Mark each page: P (works presented), C (works cold, forwarded), or P only. A standard proposal deck runs 12 to 18 pages; line extension and small jobs 6 to 10; AOR pitches up to 25.

Rules for whoever fills this in:
- If a headline cannot be substantiated from the brief, the scope output, or the library, downgrade it to what can be, or cut the page.
- Price never appears before page 9 of a presented deck unless Tree 3 Exit A was chosen deliberately (stated process, RFP, document-only decision).
- Pages marked P only must have their content carried in prose by the leave-behind proposal document. If there is no document (Tree 1 Exit C), no page may be P only.
- Speaker notes hold delivery guidance and internal reminders. They are never part of a file sent to the client; the cold-read export is a separate artefact with speaker-note content converted to on-page lines where marked.
