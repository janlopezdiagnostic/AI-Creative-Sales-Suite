# Example 1: Packaging Redesign Proposal, Full Document and Key Pages

All brands, people, and prices in this example are fictional and illustrative. Prices are taken from a fictional Scope and Pricing Architect output, reproduced exactly, to show the transcription discipline. They are not market rates.

## Inputs the skill was handed

- Discovery brief: Northfield Pantry (fictional), granola bar range, 12 SKUs in 3 families, currently invisible in fixture against two national brands. Retailer reset in March; artwork lock end of January. Decision maker: head of brand; procurement joins at contracting. Success to the client: range that reads as one system at three feet and survives a range extension next year.
- Scope output: Good 10,362.50 / Better (recommended) 12,637.50 / Best 16,212.50. Deposit 50 percent, milestones 50/30/20, 2 revision rounds per phase plus 1 artwork reconciliation round in Better and Best, source files excluded (1,500 per family), kill fee by stage.
- Case library: a 24-SKU crisps redesign (outcome citable with permission, named use allowed), a DTC coffee rebrand (imagery only, no outcome), a drinks line extension (named, no outcome recorded).
- Format inputs: sponsor presents in a meeting; document will be forwarded to the founder. Tree 1 result: BOTH.

## Arc chosen

Redesign arc: shelf moved, range did not, evidence, proof, calm plan against the reset. Proposal runs the full ten sections; deck is 14 pages.

## Proposal outline (document)

1. **Understanding.** The fixture problem in the client's words; the March reset as the fixed point; the cost of another reset cycle with a range shoppers cannot parse.
2. **What we see.** Range architecture: three families designed as twelve individuals; no blocking, no system. Evidence: the audit shelf photography from discovery, named as such.
3. **Approach.** Architecture first, then masters per family, then adaptations; gates at architecture, master approval, artwork lock; risks named: committee reconciliation (priced round already in scope), late claims copy from legal (change order rule), dieline change (change order rule).
4. **Relevant work.** Crisps redesign first (problem rhyme: range invisibility; outcome cited as the library permits). Coffee rebrand second, imagery only, text limited to deliverables. Drinks extension cut: three cases would dilute; the extension logic is proven inside the scope section instead.
5. **Scope.** Tiers transcribed. Exclusions stated: copywriting, claims substantiation and legal review, photography and licences, print costs, native files (priced per family).
6. **Team.** Lead designer (led the crisps redesign), senior designer, artworker, PM. Founder named as architecture reviewer, which is true for this studio.
7. **Timeline.** Backwards from the March reset: artwork lock end of January, legal sign-off mid January, masters approved before the holiday break, kickoff on deposit.
8. **Investment.** Tier table, exact figures, Better marked. Payment shape beside the price.
9. **Terms summary and next step.** Summary with the drafting aid flag. Next step: approve Better by reply by Friday; deposit invoice same day; kickoff date held.

## Key deck pages

- Page 2 headline: "Your range is twelve packs. Your competitors sell one system." Visual: fixture photograph from the discovery audit.
- Page 4 headline: "Architecture first is what makes January possible." Visual: range architecture diagram from the crisps project, permitted use.
- Page 5 headline: case page, crisps redesign. Speaker note: land the permitted outcome exactly as the library records it; do not round it in speech either.
- Page 11 headline: "12,637.50, and here is everything inside it." Visual: tier table. Note: reached at minute 25 of a 45 minute meeting, per Tree 3 for a committee with finance present and price in the document by agreement.

## Critique

Strengths: the insight is an observation, not a disguised concept; the document never shows Northfield's new pack, yet the client can see exactly how the studio thinks. The reconciliation risk the client was worried about is answered by a round already priced in the scope, and the proposal says so rather than conceding an extra one in the room.
Watch outs: the coffee case is imagery only; in the first draft of this example the case text said it "relaunched the brand", which is an outcome claim with no permission, and self-check 3 caught it. Also the founder forwards this to procurement eventually; the terms summary's plainness is what protects the price conversation later.
