# Self-Check: Run Before Delivering Any Outreach Set

The assistant runs these 12 checks on the finished outreach set, in order. Every check is pass or fail. A failed check means fix the set before delivery, or deliver it with the failure stated openly in the set's notes. Never silently ship a set that fails a check.

1. **Brief gate passed.** The set rests on a completed Account Research Brief with a pursue recommendation, and every signal used in copy is sourced and dated in that brief.
   - Fail if: the input was a name only, the brief says watchlist or do not pursue, or any signal in the copy has no source or date behind it.

2. **Match honestly graded.** The matched case study carries a grade (strong or partial), the grade is stated in the set's notes, and the copy's confidence matches the grade, with partial matches softened in the wording.
   - Fail if: a partial match is written up as a direct equivalent, or no citable match exists and an opener was drafted anyway.

3. **Every claim traced, nothing invented.** Each factual claim in the copy traces to the brief or the case study library. Unsupported claims are removed or marked [TO CONFIRM] and flagged to the user.
   - Fail if: any result, figure, client fact, or timeline appears that is in neither source, or an unsourced library outcome was quoted as fact.

4. **Permission levels respected.** No client is named beyond the permission level recorded in its library entry, and internal-only entries are not cited in any form, named or anonymised.
   - Fail if: an anonymised-only client is named, an internal-only entry leaks into copy, or permission was assumed rather than read from the entry.

5. **Mode declared.** The set header states connected or manual mode per source group (brief, Sales Navigator, enrichment, CRM, email), and matches what actually happened in the session.
   - Fail if: the set implies a system was checked that was only pasted, remembered, or assumed, or LinkedIn is described as anything other than manual.

6. **Drafts only.** Everything in the set is a draft. Nothing was sent, scheduled, or enrolled in any sequence or tool, and nothing in the set's notes records a touch as sent that the human has not confirmed sending.
   - Fail if: any send, scheduled send, or automation enrolment happened, or the notes blur the line between drafted and sent.

7. **Opener anatomy and length.** The opener runs trigger first, stakes second, one proof line, one ask, within the template's word budgets, with the signal's source and date held in the set's notes.
   - Fail if: the opener leads with the studio, carries two proof lines or two asks, buries the trigger, or runs materially over budget.

8. **Every follow-up adds named new value.** Each bump in the sequence names its new value (teardown insight, relevant news, seasonal hook) in its required field, sourced where it makes a factual claim.
   - Fail if: any bump's value field is empty, generic, or amounts to "just bumping", or a bump repeats an earlier touch in new words.

9. **Channel rules followed.** LinkedIn drafts meet their budgets (DM under 60 words, connection note under 300 characters with the count recorded), carry no attachments, and any warm intro ask includes the forwardable blurb and the easy-out.
   - Fail if: a DM runs long, a connection note is uncounted, a portfolio file is attached or promised as an attachment, or an intro ask gives the connector no way to decline.

10. **No fake familiarity or slop.** The copy contains no pretend prior contact, no fake Re: subject lines, no flattery about the buyer's personal life, and no slop phrases (no "just bumping", "circling back", "hope you are well" padding, or guaranteed-reply language).
    - Fail if: any line claims a relationship, meeting, or history that the brief does not evidence, or any phrase promises a reply, a meeting, or an outcome.

11. **Stop rules and opt-out handling present.** The set's notes restate the stop rules for this sequence (any reply stops it, opt-out stops it immediately and is logged the same day, out-of-office pauses it) and name where the opt-out will be recorded.
    - Fail if: the sequence is delivered as four touches to fire regardless, or no logging destination for an opt-out is named.

12. **Conflict and do-not-contact check re-run at send-prep time.** Immediately before delivery, the conflict check and do-not-contact check are run again against the fact sheet, the library entry's notes, and the CRM record as available, because conflicts and colleague conversations can appear after the brief was written.
    - Fail if: the set relies only on the brief's original conflict check, a colleague conversation with the same brand is live and unreconciled, or the target appears on any do-not-contact record.

**Scoring the check:** 12 of 12 pass: deliver. Any fail: fix and re-run the failed checks. If a fail cannot be fixed with the material available (for example, no citable case study exists), deliver only the honest output the fail permits: a do-not-send note, a softened partial-match draft with the grade stated, or a set with the failure written into its notes where the user will see it before acting.
