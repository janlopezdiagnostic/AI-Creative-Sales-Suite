# Example 1: Packaging, Strong Match on All Axes

All brands, studios, and people in this example are fictional and invented for training. Any resemblance to real companies is unintended.

**Scenario:** A packaging studio, Fieldnote Studio (fictional), wants a send-ready outreach set for Sable & Sage Pantry (fictional), a sauces and dips brand launching a new chilled range. The Account Research Brief recommends PURSUE NOW.

## Raw input (as received, messy)

> "Use the brief. Sable & Sage, new chilled dips, spring launch, new head of brand. We have loads of relevant work, just write something punchy and get it out this week. Library attached, use whatever fits."

Plus pasted material: the research brief (condensed): Recommendation PURSUE NOW. Signals: 6-SKU refrigerated dips range launching spring 2027 through a national grocery retailer (trade press, 2026-10-18, confirmed); new Head of Brand, Priya Nair (fictional), joined 2026-08-10 from an agency background (company announcement, confirmed). Hypothesis: the brand needs its ambient sauce system extended into a refrigerated fixture it has never occupied, with decisions due this quarter. Buyer: Head of Brand (brand buyer).

Plus the case-study library (condensed): Entry A, chilled dips packaging for a deli brand, 5 SKUs, category: dips, format: pots and sleeves, buyer type: head of brand, outcome: range listed nationally, permission: named client and outcome citable. Entry B, sauce range architecture, 42 SKUs, category: ambient sauces, format: labels and cases, buyer type: brand director, outcome: system shipped to shelf on a retailer deadline, permission: named client citable, outcome citable in general terms. Entry C, beverage cans, permission: internal only, not used here.

## Reasoning

- Match scoring, brief to library: Category match 3, Entry A is the same category and fixture (chilled dips). Format match 3, pots and sleeves match the target range format in the brief. Buyer match 3, Entry A was bought by a head of brand, same role as Priya Nair. Timing match 3, spring shelf date with design decisions this quarter, same pattern as Entry B.
- Overall: strong match. No claim needs softening. Every factual line in the copy below traces to the brief or to Entry A or B. No results are invented, no percentages are added, and Entry C is excluded on permission alone.
- Channel decision: email first. The buyer is named, the role is email-appropriate, and the set needs room for a teardown insight in bump 1. LinkedIn is held as a secondary touch only if email gets no response after bump 2.
- Opener discipline check: under 100 words, trigger first, one proof line only, one specific ask. Follow-ups each add new value, no repetition of the opener pitch.

## Output: outreach set for human review (drafts only, human sends)

**Email opener (78 words)**
Subject: Sable & Sage chilled launch

Hi Priya,

Saw the spring launch of the 6-SKU chilled dips range into national grocery (trade press, October). A refrigerated fixture is a different shelf problem from ambient sauces: colder light, shorter dwell time, and a system that has to hold together in pots, not jars.

We designed a 5-SKU chilled dips range that listed nationally, for a head of brand in a similar launch window.

Worth a 20-minute call next week to compare notes on the fixture shift?

Thanks,
Fieldnote Studio

**Bump 1, day 4: teardown insight**
Subject: One thing on the chilled fixture

Hi Priya, quick teardown note from the chilled work above: most sauce systems lose their blocking when they move to pots because the colour bands were designed for tall jars. In the deli dips range we rebuilt the bands for low, wide formats first, then let the labels follow. Happy to share the one-page before and after if useful. No pitch attached.

**Bump 2, day 10: new signal**
Subject: Retailer reset timing

Hi Priya, noticed the retailer confirmed its spring reset dates this week, which usually pulls artwork lock forward. On the 42-SKU sauce system we shipped, locking range architecture before individual SKUs was what kept the deadline. If your lock date has moved, a short working session now tends to save a rush later. Open to it?

**Bump 3, day 18: clean break-up**
Subject: Should I close the file?

Hi Priya, I will close the file on my side after this note. If the chilled range is already briefed, no problem at all. If timing shifts or a second pair of eyes on the system would help before lock, we are here and the dips work is the relevant place to start. Either way, good luck with the launch.

## Critique

What this example does right: the opener spends its proof budget on one matched case, not a list, and the trigger line cites its source and date so the buyer can verify it. Each bump earns its place with new value: a specific design insight, a new timing signal, then a clean exit. What to watch: strong matches tempt writers to add a second proof line and a results claim. Resist it. One proof line under 100 words converts better, and any outcome not worded exactly as the library permits it must stay out or be marked [TO CONFIRM] for the human to clear before sending.
