# Example 5: Warm Intro Path Through a Mutual Connection

All brands, studios, and people in this example are fictional and invented for training. Any resemblance to real companies is unintended.

**Scenario:** A packaging and brand studio, Fieldnote Studio (fictional), has a strong brief for a target brand, Kestrel & Co. Provisions (fictional), and a genuine mutual connection who can make an introduction. Cold outreach is possible but is the worse route.

## Raw input (as received, messy)

> "Kestrel brief is strong, pursue now. Also, Samira who used to be at Kestrel's distributor is now a consultant and knows their brand director well, she told us at an event she would happily intro if we ever wanted. Should we still just email cold? Feels wasteful not to use Samira but I do not want to impose."

Plus pasted material: the research brief (condensed): Recommendation PURSUE NOW. Signals: Kestrel & Co. Provisions will extend its preserves range by 5 SKUs for a spring retailer reset (trade press, 2026-10-12, confirmed); range architecture work is implied by the size of the extension. Buyer: Brand Director, Helen Cho (fictional), brand buyer. Hypothesis: the brand needs range extension design that holds its existing architecture under a fixed reset deadline.

Plus the case-study library (condensed): Entry A, preserves and condiments range extension, 12 SKUs, category: preserves, format: jars and labels, buyer type: brand director, outcome: extension shipped for a spring reset, permission: named client and outcome citable in general terms. Plus connection note: Samira Patel (fictional), former distributor contact, now independent consultant, knows the buyer personally, has offered an introduction in conversation. No written intro exists yet.

## Reasoning

- Match scoring: Category 3, format 3, buyer 3, timing 3. On proof alone this would be a straightforward strong-match cold email, as in Example 1.
- Channel decision, the point of this example: a warm path beats a cold path when three conditions hold, and all three hold here. One, the connection is real and recent, not a LinkedIn coincidence. Two, the connection knows the buyer personally, not just the company. Three, the connection has already signalled willingness, so the ask is a confirmation, not a cold favour. When any condition fails, default to the matched cold set instead of manufacturing a warm route.
- Cost check: a warm intro spends social capital, so the ask must be minimal, forwardable, and easy to decline. The connection should be able to forward one paragraph without editing it, and the paragraph must contain the trigger, the matched proof, and the ask, because the buyer will judge the studio from that paragraph alone.
- Sequencing: do not send the cold set in parallel. Ask for the intro first, wait a defined window (here, 7 days), and only if the intro does not land, send the cold opener with no mention of the connection. Sending both insults the connection and confuses the buyer.
- Traceability: the intro paragraph uses only brief and Entry A facts, exactly as the cold opener would. Warmth changes the route, not the evidence standard.

## Output: outreach set for human review (drafts only, human sends)

**Warm intro ask to the connection (message to Samira, not to the buyer)**
Hi Samira, thank you again for offering this at the event, and please say no if the timing is wrong, no awkwardness at all. If you are still happy to, the paragraph below is written to forward as is to Helen at Kestrel. If she says no, that is a complete answer and I will not follow up through you again.

**Forwardable paragraph (the connection forwards this unchanged)**
Hi Helen, Samira Patel suggested I write. Kestrel is extending its preserves range by 5 SKUs for the spring reset, and range extensions that size usually turn on holding the existing architecture tight under a fixed deadline. Fieldnote Studio designed a 12-SKU preserves extension for a brand director that shipped for a spring reset. They would value a 20-minute conversation to compare notes. Happy to be told it is already covered.

**Opener the sender will use if the intro lands (email, sent after Helen replies or Samira confirms the forward, 71 words)**
Subject: Thanks for the intro, Kestrel spring range

Hi Helen,

Thanks to Samira for connecting us. As she forwarded, the spring extension of 5 SKUs is the context: a range that size either reinforces the current architecture or starts to dilute it, and the reset date does not move.

Our 12-SKU preserves extension is the closest reference we have.

Would a 20-minute call next week suit to talk through the architecture questions first, before any pitch?

Thanks,
Fieldnote Studio

If the intro does not land within 7 days, the sender uses the strong-match cold set pattern from Example 1, with no reference to Samira in any message.

## Critique

What this example does right: it treats the intro as the main output, not a bonus, and it writes the forwardable paragraph to the same evidence standard as cold copy. The easy out is real, stated twice, and the follow-up burden is explicitly taken off the connection. What to watch: the most common warm-intro failure is a vague ask that forces the connection to write the pitch herself. If the paragraph needs editing by the connection, it is not ready. Second failure: thanking the connection publicly or naming her in later cold outreach. The connection's name is used only with her permission, in this route only.
