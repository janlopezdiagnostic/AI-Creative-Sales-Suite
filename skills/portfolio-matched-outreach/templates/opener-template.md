# Opener Template: First Email

One opener per buyer, built from one brief and one matched library entry. If either is missing, stop: a name-only input routes back to the Account Research Brief skill, and a brief with no honest match produces a do-not-send note, not an opener.

Total budget: 90 to 120 words in the body. Every line earns its place or it is cut. Placeholders in brackets are filled from the brief or the library only, never from memory or inference.

## Anatomy, in order

### Subject line (2 to 5 words, no clickbait, no fake Re:)

Pick one pattern:

- [Range / format] at [Brand]: for example "Line extension at [Brand]"
- [Trigger noun phrase]: for example "Autumn reset artwork"
- [Specific observation]: for example "Your new tiering on shelf"

### Line 1: Trigger line (15 to 25 words)

What changed at their brand, sourced and dated in the brief. This line is why the email exists now, not in general.

> Saw [SIGNAL: what changed, source, date] at [Brand]. [One plain consequence for packaging, brand, or range work, if the brief supports it.]

### Line 2: Stakes line (15 to 25 words)

What the trigger makes harder, riskier, or time-bound. Use only stakes the brief evidences (a reset date, an artwork deadline, a dated rule change). No invented urgency.

> With [deadline or window from the brief], [the specific pressure: artwork lock, SKU count, retailer date].

### Line 3: Matched proof line (20 to 30 words)

One library entry, graded honestly. Name the client only at or below that entry's permission level. Quote only the approved outcome wording from the entry, or mark the claim [TO CONFIRM] and flag it to the user before the draft is used.

> We did [the comparable work] for [CASE STUDY: library entry ID, named or anonymised per its permission level]: [format, SKU count, deliverable]. [Approved outcome wording, or omit the outcome entirely.]

Match grade for this opener: [Strong / Partial]. If partial, the proof line must soften: say "adjacent work" or name the gap plainly rather than implying a direct equivalent.

### Line 4: Ask line (10 to 20 words)

One ask, small and specific. A short call or a permission to send one relevant example. Never two asks, never an open-ended "thoughts?".

> Worth a 20 minute call next week to see if the same approach fits [the specific work]? Or I can send the one-page example first.

### Sign-off (under 10 words)

[First name], [Studio name]. Real contact details as the user supplies them. No fake familiarity, no "hope you are well" padding, no postscript tricks unless the postscript carries real information.

## Filled example snippet (all names fictional)

Notes on the example: the proof client is not named because CS-07 is anonymised only. The outcome quoted is the exact approved wording from the library entry. No sales figure appears, because the entry records none.

## Before this draft leaves the template

- [ ] Signal has a source and a date from the brief, and is under six months old or reactivated by a newer signal
- [ ] Proof line cites one entry ID, respects its permission level, and matches the match grade
- [ ] Every claim traces to the brief or the library; anything unsupported is marked [TO CONFIRM] and flagged
- [ ] Body is 90 to 120 words, one ask, no guaranteed reply or outcome language
- [ ] Draft only: the human reviews and sends. This template never sends anything
