# LinkedIn Template: Connection Note, DM, and InMail

LinkedIn is manual only in this suite. The user works in their own LinkedIn or Sales Navigator account, pastes or exports what they find, and the assistant drafts from that material. The assistant never scrapes, never automates browsing or messaging, never asks for LinkedIn credentials, and never sends or schedules a message. Every variant below is a draft the human reviews and sends from their own account.

Hard rules for all variants:

- **No attachments.** No portfolio PDFs, no decks, no image files. If proof matters, describe the one matched case study in words and offer to share the example by email.
- **No fake familiarity.** No "great to connect", no pretending to have met, no commenting on personal profile details (holidays, family, photos) as an opener.
- **One matched case study maximum**, cited within its library permission level, claims softened if the match grade is partial.
- **No guaranteed outcomes**, no reply-rate promises, no bulk anything: one message, one named person, one real signal.

## Variant 1: Connection request note (under 300 characters, hard limit)

Budget: 300 characters maximum including spaces. Aim for 220 to 260 so nothing is truncated. One signal, one reason, no pitch, no ask beyond the connection itself.

> Hi [First name], I work with [category] brands on packaging at [Studio]. Noticed [SIGNAL: what changed, short form] at [Brand] and thought it worth connecting. [First name]

Character count for this draft: [Count it and write the number here. Over 300 is a fail: shorten the signal, never the honesty.]

### Filled example (all names fictional, 236 characters)

> Hi Priya, I run packaging work at Studio Northlight (fictional). Saw Northbeam Pantry (fictional) announce its autumn line extension and thought it worth connecting ahead of the reset. Alex

## Variant 2: DM after connection (under 60 words)

Budget: under 60 words, total. Assumes the connection was accepted. If it was not accepted, there is no DM: do not route around that with repeated requests.

Placeholders:

- [SIGNAL: source, date, from the brief]
- [CASE STUDY: library entry ID, named only within its permission level]
- [ASK: one small ask]

> Thanks for connecting, [First name]. The [SIGNAL] is why I reached out: [one line on the packaging implication]. We did the comparable work for [CASE STUDY], [format and scale in a few words]. Worth a short call, or shall I send the one-page example by email?

### Filled example snippet (all names fictional, 51 words)

> Thanks for connecting, Priya. The autumn extension is why I reached out: eight new SKUs have to land inside your current tiering before artwork lock. We rebuilt tiering across an 18-SKU pantry range for a comparable food brand [CASE STUDY: CS-07, anonymised]. Worth a 20 minute call next week?

## Variant 3: InMail (80 to 120 words)

Budget: 80 to 120 words. InMail is for a named buyer the user has a legitimate reason to contact and no warm path to. The extra length buys a stakes line, not more pitch.

Subject (under 8 words): [Trigger noun phrase, for example "Autumn reset artwork timelines"]

> Hi [First name],
>
> [Trigger line: SIGNAL with source and date, 15 to 25 words.]
> [Stakes line: the dated pressure the trigger creates, only if the brief evidences it.]
> [Proof line: one matched entry, CASE STUDY ID, permission respected, outcome only in its approved wording or omitted.]
> [Ask line: one small ask.]
>
> [First name], [Studio name]

### Filled example snippet (all names fictional)

**Subject:** Extension artwork and the reset

> Hi Priya, Northbeam Pantry's (fictional) autumn extension was reported in trade press on 2026-09-08, with artwork lock about 10 weeks before shelf. Extensions that break the existing tiering tend to surface at the range review, when they are most expensive to fix. We rebuilt tiering across an 18-SKU pantry range for a comparable food brand [CASE STUDY: CS-07, anonymised], which kept its full listing through its review. Worth a 20 minute call on whether the same approach fits?

## Pre-send checks for any LinkedIn variant

- [ ] Drafted from user-supplied material only; mode declared manual
- [ ] Length budget met and, for the connection note, the character count is written down
- [ ] No attachment, no link to a bulk portfolio dump, no fake familiarity
- [ ] Case study named only within its permission level; no invented results
- [ ] The human sends. The assistant drafts only
