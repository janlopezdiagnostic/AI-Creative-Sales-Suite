# Template: Testimonial Ask

Use only when Tree 3 returns ASK NOW and the satisfaction evidence is in the client's own words. One ask, one message. The draft quote must be built from those recorded words; the client approves the final wording, attribution, and use, each separately.

**Subject:** A favour, only if it is easy

> [Name], [the peak moment, referenced specifically: "seeing the range land in (retailer) this week" / "your note last Tuesday"]. You said at the time: "[their exact recorded words, or a faithful short excerpt]".
>
> Would you be comfortable with us quoting you on it? To make it effortless, here is a draft in your words, shortened only for length:
>
> "[Draft quote, trimmed from their words, claiming nothing stronger than they said.]"
>
> Attribution would read: [Name, Title, Company], used on [named places only: our website and proposals]. Edit it however you like, or say not this time and I will not raise it again. Either answer is genuinely fine.

## Drafting notes (delete before sending)

- Never strengthen their language. If their words were "went really well", the quote does not become "transformed our business".
- Each attribution element (name, title, company, logo if any) is its own permission. Record each yes in the satisfaction log with the date.
- If they edit the quote into something stronger than the evidence supports, publish only the defensible version or none (see edge case 9).
- An ignored ask is an answer: log it, no nudge, next eligibility is a fresh peak at least two quarters away.
- If they say yes: thank them the day the quote goes live and send them the link. That is the whole follow-up.
