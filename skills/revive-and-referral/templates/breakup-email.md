# Template: Breakup Email

One email, sent once, only where Tree 4 returns CLEAR and the stall is silence rather than a stated no, a conflict, or an opt-out (in those cases this template is prohibited, not optional). Maximum four lines in the body. Every field comes from the deal record; if the history is not in the record, the email cannot be written.

**Subject:** Closing the loop on [project name]

> [Name], we last spoke about [project, in the client's terms] in [month of last real exchange]. I will assume the timing is wrong and close it out on my side, so it is not sitting in your inbox as another open thread.
>
> If it comes back to life, the fastest route is [lowest-friction next step: "reply to this thread" / a named call offer]. Thank you for the conversations we did have; they were genuinely useful on our side.

## Drafting notes (delete before sending)

- The assumption line must be genuinely meant. If the studio intends to keep touching the account monthly, this email is a bluff and must not be sent.
- No guilt engineering: no "I have sent three emails", no reference to effort, no passive-aggressive thanks.
- No new pitch content. The breakup email that sneaks in a case study is a sales email wearing a costume.
- After sending: tracker status moves to PARKED (with trigger watch) or CLOSED per the user's decision, and the record states "breakup sent [date]; no second breakup". A reply reopens the deal and is logged as a revival with the reply as its source.
