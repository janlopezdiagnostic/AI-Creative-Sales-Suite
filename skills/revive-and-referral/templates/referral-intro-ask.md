# Template: Referral Introduction Ask

The heaviest ask on the ladder. Use only at a live satisfaction peak (Tree 3: ASK NOW), with a named target and a reason the introduction is good or neutral for the person being introduced to. Never "anyone who might need design work". No incentive of any kind may be attached, offered, or implied.

**Subject:** An introduction, only if it sits well

> [Name], [peak moment reference, one line]. It has put me in mind of asking something specific rather than general.
>
> I would value an introduction to [named person, title, company]. The reason: [one honest line on the fit, e.g. "they are extending the range we talked about at your launch, and the work is exactly the system problem we solved together"]. I think it could be useful for them because [genuine benefit or neutral reason for the target, or "at minimum a relevant conversation"; never invent a benefit].
>
> If you are comfortable, here is a forwardable version so it costs you thirty seconds:
>
> ---
> [Name], meet [your name] from [studio]. They designed [the work the giver knows first-hand]. Given [the target's situation in one line], I thought the two of you should talk. Copying them here.
> ---
>
> And if now is not right, or they are not the right person, say so and I will not raise it again. No follow-up either way beyond a thank-you.

## After the introduction (mandatory, not template-optional)

1. Respond to the introduction thread the same day: brief, warm, no pitch in the first reply, one suggested low-friction next step.
2. Tell the giver the outcome within a week, whatever it is: meeting booked, conversation started, or not a fit (said kindly, with the reason framed as fit, never as the prospect's failing). See edge case 4.
3. Log it: giver, target, date, outcome, and the resulting pipeline entry if one is created. The entry goes to the Account Research Brief skill before any proposal work.
4. Thank the giver once more when there is news worth reporting (a meeting, a project). Then stop. A landed introduction does not create an entitlement to a second one.
