# Revival Tracker

One row per stalled or lost deal worth watching, and one line per referral ask in the second table. This tracker is the single source of truth for follow-up: one owner per account, every touch logged before it is sent, reviewed monthly. Permission status comes from the CRM or the user's record; UNKNOWN means stop until resolved (Tree 4).

**Status definitions.** Parked: no live trigger, watch continues to the review date. Reviving: a touch is drafted or sent, or a conversation has restarted. Closed: decision made (by either side) with the reasoning recorded; closed rows stay visible for two quarters so the account is not accidentally re-added, then archive.

## Monthly review checklist

1. Any watch triggers fired in the last 30 days? Grade each per Tree 2 (act, watch, drop) and record the source and date.
2. Any computed windows (reset dates, budget cycles, seasonal starts) arriving in the next 30 days? Assign the play now, not on the day.
3. Any permission changes: opt-outs, departures, acquisitions, conflicts with new clients? Update Tree 4 status before anything else moves.
4. Any duplicate owners or unlogged touches? Merge to one owner per account (edge case 19).
5. Any rows with no plausible trigger and no review date? Close them with the reasoning written down, or set the date. Clutter is a risk, not a backlog.
6. Referral side: any asks unanswered for over two quarters with a fresh peak since? Any givers still owed an outcome report? Close those loops first.
