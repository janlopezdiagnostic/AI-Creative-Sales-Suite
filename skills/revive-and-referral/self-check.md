# Self-Check: Run Before Delivering Any Revival or Referral Output

The assistant runs these 12 checks on the finished output, in order. Every check is pass or fail. A failed check means fix the output before delivery, or deliver it with the failure stated openly. Never silently ship an output that fails a check. Checks 1 and 2 are absolute: a failure on either stops the output entirely.

1. **Contact permission verified.** Tree 4 was run for every contact named in the output, against the CRM record or the user's supplied history, and the result is stated in the output.
   - Fail if: permission is assumed, unrecorded, or marked unknown and the output still drafts a touch. Absolute stop if any contact has ever opted out.

2. **No closed account is being worked.** No deal lost on a genuine conflict, and no account the client themselves closed, appears as a revival or referral target.
   - Fail if: a conflict loss is reframed as a stall anywhere in the output. Absolute stop.

3. **Diagnosis present and evidenced.** Every revival candidate carries one primary stall cause, with the supporting record (client words, dates) named.
   - Fail if: a touch is drafted for a deal whose stall cause is "unknown" with no history-recovery step.

4. **Every trigger is sourced and dated.** Each trigger cited names its source and the event date (not just the alert date), and carries a grade (confirmed, reported, unverified) per Tree 2.
   - Fail if: any trigger reads "recently", "apparently", or "is said to" without a dated source behind it.

5. **Trigger matches the block.** The trigger relied on actually changes the diagnosed blocking condition (budget, champion, timing, need).
   - Fail if: the touch is built on generic good news unrelated to why the deal stalled.

6. **Nothing invented about the relationship.** Every reference to past contact, past meetings, past words, or past work matches the deal history supplied. No shared history, attendance, or conversation is claimed that the record does not show.
   - Fail if: any line would be false if the client forwarded it to a colleague with the full thread.

7. **Value is real.** Each revival touch carries a value-add item (teardown, trend note, permitted case result, introduction, or resource) that would be useful to the recipient even if the deal never revives.
   - Fail if: the touch reduces to "just checking in", "circling back", or a restated pitch with a trigger pasted on top.

8. **Cadence respected.** The output does not schedule a third unanswered touch without a new trigger, does not repeat a breakup email, and does not contact a budget-frozen account before the window they named.
   - Fail if: the plan contradicts a date or a limit already recorded in the tracker.

9. **Satisfaction evidence is real, fresh, and theirs.** Every referral ask rests on satisfaction evidence in the client's own words, an acknowledged outcome, or a repeat purchase, dated within the last year, with no unresolved later problem.
   - Fail if: the evidence is the seller's impression, second-hand praise, or older than the last delivery issue.

10. **Ask is single, matched, and escapable.** One ask per client per occasion, matched to the evidence strength on the playbook ladder, containing a genuine low-cost way to decline, with the effort pre-done (draft, link, blurb, named targets).
    - Fail if: asks are stacked, the decline path is performative ("no worries at all!!" followed by a scheduled follow-up), or the giver is left to do the drafting or the targeting.

11. **No incentives, no inflation.** The output offers no personal incentive, kickback, or policy-breaching gift, and no testimonial or case result says anything stronger than what the client approved or what their recorded words support.
    - Fail if: any quote, logo use, or outcome claim appears without its permission recorded in the satisfaction log.

12. **Mode declared, drafts labelled, logging ready.** The header declares connected or manual mode per tool honestly. Every client-facing text is marked as a draft for the user's confirmation. A tracker or CRM update accompanies the output so the action, once taken, is on record.
    - Fail if: the output implies a source, inbox, or CRM was checked that was only pasted or assumed, or if a sent touch would leave no record behind it.
