# Example 3: The Founder Call

All brands, people, and details in this example are fictional and invented for training.

## Input (notes after the call, seller's shorthand tidied)

"Founder, small skincare brand, DTC, wants packaging for launch range, 4 products. Loved our work for [another founder]. Asked early 'what would you do with the brand', I gave principles only. Budget: said 'we have raised enough to do this properly', pushed twice, no number. Launch tied to a retail meeting in September, wants shelf-ready packs for the meeting, not full production run necessarily. Current designer is a freelancer who 'cannot do structural'. She decides everything, partner handles ops but not on call. Asked if we can start next week."

## Skill output (extraction summary and coaching notes)

**Mode: EXTRACTION from seller notes. Confidence: moderate (notes, not transcript).**

**Brief highlights:** Trigger: September retail meeting; packs needed as shelf-ready presentation standard, production quantities undecided `[NOT STATED]` whether the meeting needs final print or comps. Deliverables: packaging for 4 launch products, structural included (incumbent freelancer cannot do structural; that gap is the stated reason for the call). Identity: `[NOT STATED]` whether identity is in scope; the brand exists and sells DTC, so probably packs onto an existing identity, confirm before scoping. Decision process: founder decides; ops partner not met, materials/production views unknown. Budget: shape only ("raised enough to do this properly"), tested twice, no figure: Budget 1, marked `[NOT STATED]` with the founder's own words quoted. Timeline: September meeting real and dated; "start next week" is enthusiasm, not a schedule.

**Scores:** Fit 3, Budget 1, Timing 2 (dated driver, but the required standard for September, comps vs production, is unresolved), Access 3 (the decider was on the call). Verdict: **PAID DISCOVERY FIRST**: a two-week, fixed-fee discovery that confirms scope shape (identity in or out, production standard for September), the print route, and a real budget conversation anchored to a costed scope outline. Founders often find money once they can see what the money buys; discovery manufactures that moment honestly.

**Coaching notes for the seller:** Giving principles only, not brand strategy, was correct: the founder asked the free-strategy question at minute five and will ask again. The risk to manage is the moving number: "properly funded" in a good week becomes "tight" in a bad one; the discovery fee being paid is the test that separates the two. Get the ops partner on the discovery call; structural and materials decisions made without ops get unmade later at the studio's expense.

## Critique

Founder calls are the easiest to enjoy and the easiest to mis-score: warmth and decisiveness masquerade as qualification. The extraction keeps Access at 3, because the decider really was present, while refusing to let that inflate Budget, which remains the emptiest field on the page.
