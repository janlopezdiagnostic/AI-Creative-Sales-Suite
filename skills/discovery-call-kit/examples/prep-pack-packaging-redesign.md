# Example 1: Prep Pack for a Packaging Redesign Call

All brands, people, and details in this example are fictional and invented for training.

## Input (what the skill was given)

Research brief (fictional): Solstice Drinks Co, seasonal sparkling drinks, 14 SKUs, retailer reset March 2027 with submissions December 2026, new brand manager started August 2026. Fit scores 3/2/3, verdict PURSUE. Call booked: 40 minutes with the brand manager and "someone from supply chain". The invite promised "a chat about the range and your approach". Fact sheet: studio has 3 beverage packaging case studies, floor is mid five figures, no category conflict.

## Prep pack (output)

**Mode: PREP. Based on: research brief dated 18 Oct 2026 (fictional), fact sheet on file.**

**Hypothesis in one line:** The March reset and the December submission deadline mean artwork scope must be agreed within weeks; this call tests whether the brand manager can commit the business or only recommend.

**The three things this call must establish:**
1. The real deliverables count: 14 SKUs confirmed, or has the range changed since the listings the brief used?
2. Who signs: brand manager's approval limit and who sits above her.
3. The production chain: printer, dieline ownership, artwork lock date implied by the December submission.

**Question route (brand director pattern, adapted for a junior buyer):** trigger first (what did the retailer say in the reset conversation), then the work (which SKUs lead, which are seasonal), then production (printer and dielines), then the commercial frame (budget shape, who approves, who else is quoting). Proof points to have open: the studio's 12-SKU beverage relaunch case study and one shelf photo set. Do not open the fashion portfolio; irrelevant proof reads as padding.

**Traps to avoid:** the invite says "your approach", which invites a free strategy presentation. Approach stays at principle level until the deliverables count exists. No ballpark if she asks "roughly what would 14 SKUs cost"; answer with the structure (architecture base plus per-SKU artwork plus production support) and the promise of a scoped number within 48 hours of the call. Do not criticise the incumbent studio, unnamed in the brief, by guessing at their work on current packs.

**Planned close:** "If the shape is what I think it is, our next step is a scoped proposal by Friday, which needs the SKU list and dieline status from your side by Wednesday. Can we agree that now?" Fallback if the signer is not identified: book the second call with the signer invited, date agreed on this call.

## Critique

The pack is built from the brief without inflating it: every question traces to a gap the brief named (junior buyer, unverified budget, production unknown). Note what it does not do: it does not script charm, and it does not prepare concepts. Prep that ends with "show them ideas" has failed before the call starts.
