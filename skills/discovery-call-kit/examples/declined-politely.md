# Example 6: Declined Politely

All brands, people, and details in this example are fictional and invented for training.

## Input (extraction facts)

A national meal-kit brand (fictional) invited the studio to "pitch" for a packaging refresh. On the call it emerged: eight studios are being asked for concepts, the concepts are due in ten days, there is no budget figure and the marketing manager running the process said budget questions were "for after we pick a winner", the winner's fee is "to be negotiated", and a previous round with another brand in the group ended with no studio appointed and the concepts "kept on file". The brand is famous. The seller wants to pursue because of the logo.

## Skill output (verdict and the decline)

**Mode: EXTRACTION + verdict. Scores:** Fit 2 (the work itself suits the studio), Budget 0 (unstated and structurally undiscoverable: no figure exists until after concepts), Timing 1 (ten-day concept deadline is urgency about the harvest, not about a launch; no shelf driver was named in the call), Access 1 (marketing manager only; no route to a signer offered). **Total 4, and Tree 1's extractive-process gate fires before the total even matters.**

**Verdict: DECLINE POLITELY (process).** The paid discovery route was offered on the call and refused. Famous is not a qualification dimension. The arithmetic of this process: eight studios times concept work, one possible fee, fee negotiated after maximum leverage has been surrendered, and a group precedent of appointing nobody. The studio's expected return is negative before travel time.

**The decline (drafted for the seller's approval):** "Thank you for including us. We have talked it over and we are going to step back from this round. Our model is to do evaluation work as a paid discovery, because concepts produced cold, without the range audit and the production picture, do not represent what we would actually do for the brand, and it would not be fair to judge us, or the other studios, on them. If the process changes, or a future project runs on a scoped basis, we would be glad to be considered, and I will keep an eye on the range with real interest."

**What happens to the account:** Watchlist, not blacklist. Review trigger: a new brand lead, or any future approach with a brief and a budget conversation permitted. The reasoning is filed in the brief so next year's seller does not have to re-litigate the logo argument from scratch.

## Critique

Everything in this example is designed to pressure a yes: fame, deadline, a room full of competitors, a seller arguing for the logo in internal messages. The skill's job is to be the colleague who reads the process instead of the poster. Note that the decline protects the relationship deliberately; "your process is extractive" is the analysis, never the message.
