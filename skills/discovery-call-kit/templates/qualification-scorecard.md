# Template: Qualification Scorecard

Score each dimension 0-3 from the evidence in the notes or transcript only. Every score needs its evidence line; a score without a quote or a specific stated fact is a guess, and guesses get scored as the lower value.

## Fit (portfolio proof and desire for the work)
- 3: Deliverables and category match named studio proof; the work is work the studio wants more of; no conflict.
- 2: Adjacent proof, credible stretch; or fit is real but part of the scope is work the studio does adequately, not well.
- 1: Weak or unproven match; the studio would be learning on the client's money.
- 0: No match, a conflict with a current client, or the deliverable is something the studio does badly. (0 here ends the evaluation: Tree 1, Exit D.)
