# Template: Question Bank Quick Reference

One page, for the desk during the call. Full reasoning is in `playbook.md`.

## Open
- "My aim is to understand what is driving this and whether we are right for it. What do you need from the next [40] minutes?"

## Trigger
- "What has changed that makes this a now project?"
- "What happens if it does not happen, or happens badly?"
- "Is it tied to a retailer conversation, a launch, or a competitor?"

## The work
- "Talk me through the range as it stands: SKUs, formats, languages, including what is coming next quarter."
- "Which phases do you see: strategy, naming, structural, artwork, production support, guidelines?"
- "What is explicitly out?"

## Production (packaging)
- "Who prints today: flexo, litho, gravure, digital? Fixed or open?"
- "Do dielines exist, and who owns them?"
- "Any material or sustainability commitments already made: substrates, recyclability, EPR exposure?"
- "Who approves colour and signs proofs on your side?"

## Creative expectations
- "Two packs or brands you wish were yours: what is it about them?"
- "What would make a concept dead on arrival?"
- "How are creative decisions made here: one taste, committee, testing?"

## Commercial frame
- "How has the business thought about cost: set budget, a range, or not yet?"
- "What did the last comparable project cost, roughly?"
- "If we came in at twice your number, what would happen?"
- "If you love the proposal, what happens between there and a start date? Who else sees it? Who signs at this value?"
- "Who else are you talking to, and what does their process look like?"

## Past experience
- "What did the last studio get right? What frustrated you?"
- "Why is this not going to them?"
- "What would make you fire a studio in month one?"

## Pivots (verbatim rescue lines)
- Price on the spot: "I can give you the structure now and a scoped number by [date]. A number today would be a guess wearing a suit."
- Concepts requested: "Concepts cold would not represent what we would actually do. The honest version is a paid discovery: [scope], fixed fee, credited if we proceed."
- Impossible date: "Let us do the arithmetic together: shelf date minus print minus artwork lock. Here are the three levers: scope, sequence, rush."
- Signer absent: "Can we present the proposal to them together? I would rather hear their questions first-hand."

## Close (always)
- Recap in their words. Name the next step. Agree the date on the call.
