# Example 2: Brand Identity and Packaging Starter Scope

All brands, people, and prices in this example are fictional and illustrative. Prices are calculated from the fictional rate sheet shown in this example, not offered as market rates.

## The studio (fictional rate sheet excerpt)
Hartwell Studio. Currency USD, prices exclude tax.
- Creative director 950/day, strategist 800/day, senior designer 750/day, designer 550/day, artworker 450/day, project manager 500/day
- Included revision rounds: 2 per phase. Deposit 50 percent. Milestones 50 / 30 / 20
- Source files: excluded, priced at 1,500 per deliverable family (identity counts as one family, packaging as one)

## Messy input (discovery call notes, raw)
"Fernlight Skincare, two founders, launching DTC first, retail later 'hopefully'. Four products planned but only the serum is formulated. No name issues, name is final. They have a moodboard, competitor set is premium minimal brands. They keep saying 'full brand' and 'packaging for the range'. Budget mentioned once: 'we were hoping around ten'. Launch 'spring, no date'. Photographer friend may shoot. They want everything ready so they can 'just add products'."

## Reasoning
1. "The range" is not countable today: only 1 of 4 products exists. Scoping 4 SKUs would price fiction. The scope covers identity plus the hero SKU properly, with the other 3 products priced as a defined adaptation rate they can trigger later (decision tree 1: fixed fee for what is countable, stated rate for what is not yet).
2. "Full brand" needs decomposing: strategy, identity system, packaging master, guidelines. Offered as tiers, because the founders' budget signal ("around ten") sits between a lean identity-plus-one-pack and the full system.
3. "Just add products later" is a guidelines and template need. That is the Best tier, sold honestly as the thing that makes later additions cheap.
4. The photographer friend is an asset risk: photography is excluded, with a note that if their photography falls through, art direction support is a change order.
5. No hard date: no rush. A soft "spring" is written as an assumption, not a promise.

## The maths (internal, shown here for training)
Better tier:
- Strategy and positioning: strategist 3 days (2,400) + creative director 1 day (950) = 3,350
- Identity system: senior designer 6 days (4,500) + creative director 1.5 days (1,425) = 5,925
- Packaging master, hero SKU: senior designer 3 days (2,250) + artworker 1 day (450) = 2,700
- Project management: 2 days = 1,000
- Better total: 12,975
Good tier: lean strategy 1 day (800); identity senior designer 4 days (3,000) + creative director 1 day (950) = 3,950; packaging master senior designer 2 days (1,500) + artworker 0.5 day (225) = 1,725; project management 1 day (500). Total 6,975.
Best tier: Better + naming architecture check (strategist 2 days, 1,600) + brand and packaging guidelines (senior designer 3 days, 2,250) + artwork for the remaining 3 SKUs at the adaptation rate (per SKU designer 0.5 day (275) + artworker 0.25 day (112.50) = 387.50; x 3 = 1,162.50). Best total: 12,975 + 1,600 + 2,250 + 1,162.50 = 17,987.50.

## Output: tiered scope (client facing excerpt)
**Good, 6,975.** Positioning summary (one workshop, one document), identity system (logo suite, colour, type, layout principles), packaging master for the serum. Two revision rounds per phase. Right for launch if the remaining products will follow the serum's format closely.
**Better (recommended), 12,975.** Full strategy phase (audience, competitor audit, messaging hierarchy), identity system with more exploration, packaging master with structural format guidance for your box supplier, project management throughout.
**Best, 17,987.50.** Everything in Better, plus naming architecture for future products, brand and packaging guidelines, and artwork for the remaining 3 products at the agreed adaptation rate when they are formulated.
Not included: copywriting, photography, font and image licences, print and production costs, trademark clearance (you need your own attorney's search; drafting aid note, have counsel review any IP language), ecommerce build, source files (1,500 per family if required).
Future SKU rate, locked in this proposal: 387.50 per adaptation on the existing master format.

## SOW excerpt
"1. Deliverables: strategy document; identity system as listed; print ready packaging artwork for 1 SKU (Fernlight Renewal Serum, 30ml). Additional SKUs are outside this agreement and will be quoted at the adaptation rate stated in the proposal. 7. Intellectual property: on full payment the client receives the final identity files and packaging artwork for the products listed. Drafting aid, have counsel review before use."

## Critique
Strengths: the proposal refuses to price products that do not exist, while still answering the founders' real need ("just add products") with a locked adaptation rate and the guidelines tier. The budget signal was respected by building an honest 6,975 tier rather than discounting the 12,975 scope.
Watch outs: "retail later" will change packaging requirements (barcode, claims, shelf standout). The scope notes DTC first; if a retailer listing lands mid-project, that is a change order conversation, and the SOW assumptions should say the artwork is specified for DTC presentation. Trademark clearance is flagged but founders often skip it; the studio's protection is the written flag, which is present.
