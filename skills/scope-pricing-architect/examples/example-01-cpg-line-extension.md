# Example 1: CPG Packaging Line Extension, Full Scope

All brands, people, and prices in this example are fictional and illustrative. Prices are calculated from the fictional rate sheet shown in this example, not offered as market rates.

## The studio (fictional rate sheet excerpt)
Hartwell Studio. Currency USD, prices exclude tax.
- Creative director 950/day, strategist 800/day, senior designer 750/day, designer 550/day, artworker 450/day, project manager 500/day
- Included revision rounds: 2 per phase. Additional round: priced per phase at quoting
- Rush multiplier: 1.5x on affected phases. Deposit 50 percent. Milestones 50 / 30 / 20
- Minimum booking: half a day per role for any booked item (per-SKU figures below are the build-up of one adaptation block, not separate bookings)
- Source files: excluded, priced at 1,500 per design family

## Messy input (email from the client, verbatim style)
"Hi, we are extending the Northfield Pantry granola bar range. Currently 4 flavours sell well, we are adding 8 more, so 12 SKUs total, 3 design families (Classic, Protein, Kids). Needs to hit the March retailer reset, artwork locked by end of January. Our printer is GreenLeaf, dielines attached for the 4 current SKUs, the rest are same format. Legal needs to review claims on the Protein family. Marketing wants 'flexibility' on rounds because our VP likes to tweak. Can you price the lot?"

## Reasoning
1. Deliverables are countable: 12 SKUs, 3 families, 1 format. Fixed fee model (decision tree 1, Exit A).
2. "Flexibility on rounds" is a revision risk. The scope will define a round and cap included rounds at 2 per phase per the rate sheet; the committee reconciliation risk gets priced into Better and Best via an included extra artwork round.
3. Legal review of claims is the client's responsibility; the scope says so, and re-artwork from late legal changes is a change order (edge case 13).
4. Dielines exist for the current format; the scope assumes the same dieline for all 12, stated as an assumption. A supplier dieline change is a change order.
5. Deadline is workable without rush: no multiplier, but the date is written in as a milestone dependency on client feedback turnaround.

## The maths (internal, shown here for training)
- Architecture and range organisation: strategist 1 day (800) + creative director 0.5 day (475) = 1,275
- Master design, 3 families: per family senior designer 2 days (1,500) + creative director 0.5 day (475) = 1,975; x 3 = 5,925
- Adaptations, 9 further SKUs: per SKU designer 0.5 day (275) + artworker 0.25 day (112.50) = 387.50; x 9 = 3,487.50 (booked as one block: designer 4.5 days and artworker 2.25 days in total, so each role's booking clears the half-day minimum)
- Consolidated reconciliation round at artwork stage: designer 0.5 day (275) + artworker 0.5 day (225) = 500
- Print proofing support: artworker 1 day = 450
- Project management: 2 days = 1,000
- Better total: 12,637.50
- Good is Better minus the architecture phase (1,275), minus the reconciliation round (500), minus 1 project management day (500) = 10,362.50
- Best is Better plus guidelines (senior designer 3 days = 2,250), plus a second proofing round (artworker 1 day = 450), plus the rollout support block (mixed-role launch support) itemised as designer 1 day (550) + artworker 0.5 day (225) + project manager 0.5 day (250, booked at the half-day minimum) = 1,025; Best total: 16,362.50

## Output: tiered scope (client facing excerpt)
**Good, 10,362.50.** Everything in Better except: no strategy and architecture phase (studio works to your written range plan), no included reconciliation round, project management at 1 day, 2 revision rounds per phase. Choose this only if the range architecture is already settled internally.
**Better (recommended), 12,637.50.** Architecture phase, master design for 3 families, adaptation of 9 further SKUs, 2 revision rounds per phase plus 1 included consolidated reconciliation round at artwork stage (priced in the maths above), one proofing round with GreenLeaf, project management throughout.
**Best, 16,362.50.** Everything in Better, plus packaging guidelines for future SKUs (senior designer 3 days), a second proofing round, and a rollout support block for launch questions (itemised in the maths above).
Not included in any tier: copywriting, claims substantiation and legal review (yours), photography, illustration, font and image licences, print costs, native source files (available at 1,500 per design family).
Terms summary: 50 percent deposit, 30 percent at artwork approval, 20 percent before final files. Kill fee per SOW stages. Changes quoted and approved in writing before work starts.
Assumptions: 12 SKUs on the attached dieline; consolidated feedback from one named decision maker within 3 working days per round.

## SOW excerpt
"1. Deliverables: master artwork for 3 design families (Classic, Protein, Kids); adapted artwork for 9 additional SKUs as listed in Appendix A; print ready files per SKU to the GreenLeaf dieline supplied at signing (a supplier dieline change after signing is a change order). Full exclusions per Section 5 of this SOW. 2. Revisions: 2 rounds per phase, a round being one consolidated set of feedback from the client's named decision maker, plus 1 reconciliation round at artwork phase."

## Critique
Strengths: the SKU count is a number in the scope, so growth prices itself; the "flexibility" ask was answered with a structure (a reconciliation round) instead of either a refusal or unlimited rounds.
Watch outs: the dieline assumption is load bearing; if GreenLeaf changes the format, the change order conversation must happen the same week, not at proof stage. The example also assumes one decision maker; if the VP tweaking bypasses that person, edge case 9 applies and the studio must use the pause it wrote into the scope.
