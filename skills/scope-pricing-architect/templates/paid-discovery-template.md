# Paid Discovery Template (the anti spec work offer)

Use this template when a brief cannot be priced honestly, or when a client asks for free creative routes as part of a pitch. Discovery is a product with its own deliverable: the findings and the scoped plan. It is not a discounted sample of the project, and it is not a paid pitch. Drafting aid, have counsel review before use, particularly the credit, intellectual property, and payment sections.

## Why discovery, stated to the client
[Two or three factual lines naming the open questions that make the main project unpriceable today: SKU or variant count, range architecture, format or supplier constraints, budget, decision structure. These are the client's own unanswered questions, named without blame. Example shape: "The range is described as approximately 20 SKUs, the architecture across the two formats is undecided, and there is no confirmed budget. Any creative route drawn today would be a guess, and guesses at this stage become expensive changes later."]

## What discovery includes
- [Audit: current range, category, and competitor presentation, shelf and or online, as applicable]
- [Range or brand architecture: how the line is organised and what differentiates each part]
- [Format and production feasibility review against the supplier's stated constraints]
- [Deliverables inventory for the full project, counted: SKUs, variants, formats, phases]
- [A scoped, fixed price proposal for the full project, with dates worked back from [hard deadline or launch date]]
- [One working session to present findings and the plan: duration, attendees]

## What discovery does not include
- Finished creative routes for the project. [One concept direction may be shown as an illustration of the architecture where the studio chooses: include or delete this line, and mark the choice, before sending. [TO CONFIRM: illustration included yes / no]]
- Naming generation, unless listed above
- Any production files, artwork, or supplier ready output
- Trademark or legal clearance of any name or claim (the studio can support screening, clearance is counsel's work)

## Credit against the full project
- If the client signs the full project proposal arising from this discovery within [60] days [TO CONFIRM] of the discovery presentation, [100 percent / stated percentage] [TO CONFIRM] of the discovery fee is credited against the project fee.
- The credit applies once, to that project only, for the same client entity and substantially the same scope presented. It is not transferable, not refundable, and has no cash value if the project does not proceed.
- If the project does not proceed, the discovery deliverables listed above remain the client's to use, on full payment of the discovery fee. Drafting aid, have counsel review before use.
