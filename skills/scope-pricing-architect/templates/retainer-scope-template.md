# Retainer Scope Template (range guardianship and ongoing design support)

Drafting aid, have counsel review before use. This template structures a monthly retainer built on the playbook Part 2 model: a defined pool of days, a defined overage rate, a stated rollover rule, and a quarterly review. A retainer is a capacity reservation, not a discount scheme, and the rates are the studio's standard day rates, unchanged.

## 4. How the block works
- Requests come from the client's named contact [name] to the studio project manager [name]. Work requested through any other route is redirected there before it starts.
- The project manager confirms the estimated days for each request before work starts, and flags at once if a request would exceed the remaining block.
- A usage note accompanies each invoice: days used, by role, and what they produced.
- Rollover: unused days [do not roll over / roll over for one month up to a cap of [number] days] [TO CONFIRM, choose one and delete the other]. The fee reserves capacity the studio cannot sell elsewhere, which is why the rule is stated here rather than negotiated month by month.

## 5. Overage
- Work beyond the monthly block is charged at the standard day rates in section 1, half day minimum. Retainer rates are the standard rates; there is no separate overage premium and no retainer discount [adjust only if the rate sheet says otherwise].
- Overage is quoted in writing and approved in writing before the work starts. Unapproved overage is not done.
- Rush requests within the block follow the rate sheet rush terms [multiplier and definition] [TO CONFIRM].

## 6. Quarterly review
- At the end of each quarter, both parties review: days used against the block, work produced, the range plan, and the next quarter's forecast.
- Either side may propose resizing the block, or changing its role mix, for the following quarter. Changes take effect from the next quarter and are recorded in a short written note signed by both parties.
- Usage trigger: if usage runs under [70] percent of the block for two consecutive months, the studio raises resizing at the review itself, rather than letting the client pay for unused capacity unremarked.
