# Decision Trees: Project to Retainer Converter

Run these in order. Tree 1 gates everything: its verdict decides whether the other trees run at all.

## Tree 1. Project outcome: pitch, repair, or wait

START: The project has shipped (or its main phase has). Assess the outcome from the project record and the client's own words.

- IF the launch or delivery landed as agreed, results evidence exists, and the client's sign-off language is positive
  -> STRONG. Proceed: recap, map, design, pitch in the normal window (two to six weeks post-launch).
- IF delivery landed but with real blemishes (a studio error acknowledged, a delay with shared causes, a result that is genuinely mixed)
  -> MIXED. Proceed differently:
     - The recap leads with the repair paragraph (playbook Part 1).
     - Has the repair conversation already happened and been accepted by the client?
       - YES -> Pitch a smaller retainer scoped only to the work that demonstrably went well (for example artwork operations without strategy). Mark the shape "starter, one quarter, review to expand".
       - NO -> Do the repair conversation first. Output of this skill is the repair plan and a review date, not a pitch.
- IF the project is in dispute (contested invoices, unacknowledged errors, responsibility argued), OR the launch failed and the studio's part in that is unresolved
  -> POOR / OPEN. STOP. No recap, no retainer design.
     - Output: a repair plan (what is acknowledged, what is offered, who speaks, by when) and a dated review point at least one full delivery cycle after repair.
     - EXIT: return to this tree only when the dispute is closed in writing.
- IF the outcome was strong but the client organisation is mid-freeze, mid-acquisition, or the champion has just left
  -> STRONG BUT WAIT. Build the recap and map now (evidence is perishable), set the pitch for the dated stability point, and record the trigger. See Tree 4.

Defined exits: PITCH (proceed to Tree 2) / PITCH SMALLER (proceed to Tree 2 with starter constraint) / REPAIR FIRST (no pitch output) / WAIT (build assets, date the pitch).

## Tree 2. Retainer structure choice

START: Tree 1 returned PITCH or PITCH SMALLER, and the expansion map has at least one evidenced route.

- Q1: Is the dominant ongoing need coherence: many hands touching one range or system, drift the main risk?
  - YES -> GUARDIANSHIP base (senior-weighted small block, checks and updates).
- Q2: Is the dominant need throughput: a forecast flow of new work moving through stages (NPD, extensions, refreshes)?
  - YES -> PIPELINE block (designer and strategist weighted), sized from the flow's cadence.
- Q3: Is the dominant need capacity inside the client's own rhythm: their queue, their standups, their tools?
  - YES -> EMBEDDED DAYS (named people, named days), with a capacity cap agreed so one client cannot swallow the studio.
- IF Q1 plus either Q2 or Q3 applies (the common packaging case)
  -> HYBRID: guardianship base + flex drawn as pre-approved overage at standard rates for peaks.
- IF Tree 1 returned PITCH SMALLER
  -> Override: choose the single structure matching the work that went well, smallest honest block, one quarter, whatever the answers above.

Sizing rule for every branch: days per month by role come from the expansion map's cadence evidence, priced from the rate sheet. If the evidence supports fewer days than the structure's minimum sensible block (half day units, a review rhythm), the honest output is "no retainer yet", plus the door-open plan. Do not inflate the block to make the structure look real.

Defined exits: GUARDIANSHIP / PIPELINE / EMBEDDED / HYBRID / NO RETAINER YET.

## Tree 3. Client size and process fit

START: A structure is chosen. Check how this client can actually buy and run it.

- Micro or founder-led client (signer is the user of the work):
  -> Light process: monthly block, simple usage note, verbal approval followed by email confirmation for overage. Pitch directly to the founder. Watch: cash-flow seasonality; offer the quarterly review as their safety valve.
- Mid-size client, marketing-led (a champion, a budget holder, light procurement):
  -> Standard process: champion brief required, finance questions pre-answered, proposal addressed to the budget holder with the champion as carrier. Overage approval in writing from the named contact.
- Large or group client, procurement-governed:
  -> Full process: expect vendor onboarding, rate card submission, framework or panel rules, and payment terms pressure. The retainer must fit their supplier model or it dies regardless of the champion's enthusiasm. Never let procurement be the first audience: champion and budget holder first, procurement with the recap evidence in hand. If their model demands terms the rate sheet forbids (for example rates benchmarked down as a condition), escalate to the user as a founder decision, do not negotiate it inside the output.
- Client with a capable in-house team:
  -> Position as the layer the in-house team cannot be: system memory, senior review, overflow at peaks, specialist production depth. Design the block around their gaps from the expansion map, and name the in-house lead as a collaborator in the proposal, never as the problem the retainer solves.

Defined exits: LIGHT / STANDARD / FULL PROCESS / IN-HOUSE COMPLEMENT, each setting the kit contents in SKILL.md Step 5.

## Tree 4. Timing: now, this quarter, or decline to pitch yet

START: Assets are ready (recap, map, design). Choose when the pitch lands.

- Launch was two to six weeks ago, results evidence is fresh, no freeze or dispute, champion in place
  -> NOW. Book the meeting inside the window.
- The client's planning or budget round closes on a known date
  -> THIS QUARTER, sequenced: recap conversation now, formal proposal papered to land before the planning freeze. Name both dates in the plan.
- A seasonal peak or retailer reset is the sizing evidence
  -> Count backwards from the artwork lock date (commonly eight to twelve weeks before shelf, verify per retailer). The pitch lands early enough that the retainer can actually staff the peak; pitching inside the lock window makes the first month a rush problem and poisons the structure.
- Champion departing, freeze active, restructure underway, or Tree 1 said repair first
  -> DECLINE TO PITCH YET. Output: the reason in one line, the dated re-entry trigger (named event or date), and the keep-warm action for the interval. Record it in the CRM with confirmation (tools.md).
- No evidenced ongoing work at any horizon
  -> DECLINE, permanently for now: thank-you and door-open plan only. See SKILL.md, When NOT to use this skill.

Defined exits: NOW / THIS QUARTER (two dates named) / DECLINE YET (trigger dated) / DECLINE (door-open plan only).
