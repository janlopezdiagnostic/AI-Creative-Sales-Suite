## 5. Quarterly review and exit (summary)

If usage runs under 70 percent of the block for two consecutive months, we will raise resizing at the review ourselves. Either party may end the retainer at the end of a quarter by giving written notice, on the terms set out in the contractual scope. Drafting aid, have counsel review before use.
