# Pitch Meeting Agenda Template

A 45 minute meeting to decide on a retainer proposal. Send this agenda with the proposal and the results recap at least [number] working days before the meeting. Fill every bracketed field.

Meeting: [title]  |  Date and time: [date, time, timezone]  |  Place or link: [location]
Attendees: [names and roles, client side] / [names and roles, studio side]
Documents sent in advance: Retainer Proposal [reference], Results Recap [reference], Expansion Map summary [reference]

## Agenda and timings (45 minutes total)
| Time | Item | Led by |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 min | Purpose and decision needed. One sentence: we are here to decide on [block summary] starting [date]. | [studio lead] |
| 5 to 15 min | Results recap. What shipped, what the evidence shows, questions on the numbers only. | [studio lead] |
| 15 to 25 min | Ongoing needs. The confirmed expansion routes, volume, and timing triggers. Client corrects the map where it is wrong. | [client contact] |
| 25 to 35 min | The proposal. Block table, how the block works, overage, rollover choice still to confirm, quarterly review, exit. | [studio lead] |
| 35 to 42 min | Questions: finance, procurement, routing, start date. Anything unanswered is logged with an owner and a date, live in the meeting. | [all] |
| 42 to 45 min | The decision, or the fallback below. Confirm who decides, and by when, out loud before the meeting ends. | [client decision maker] |

## Who brings what
- Studio brings: [the proposal and recap as above; the usage and sizing basis in days; one worked overage example at standard rates; the contractual retainer scope, ready to walk through if asked]
- Client brings: [forthcoming range or season plans that affect sizing; finance or procurement requirements, such as purchase order process and payment terms [TO CONFIRM]; the name of the person who will route requests]
- [TO CONFIRM: any client specific item, for example legal review requirements for the contractual scope]

## Decision asked for at the end
"Do you approve the block of [total days] days per month at [monthly fee] per month, at the studio's standard rates, starting [date], with the rollover choice and remaining points recorded in the contractual scope for signature?"
Possible answers and what each triggers:
- Yes: contractual scope issued by [date], first invoice on [date or trigger].
- Yes, with a resized block: new size agreed in the room and minuted, proposal reissued by [date].
- Not yet: move to the fallback, do not re pitch in the room.

## Fallback if no decision
No decision is a normal outcome, not a failed meeting. Before leaving: agree a dated follow up, [date, within 10 working days unless the client sets another date], name the single open question blocking the decision and its owner, and agree the studio sends a one page summary of this meeting by [date]. If the follow up date passes with no decision and no new date, the proposal is recorded as Dormant with its re entry trigger, and the studio stops chasing. One reminder before the follow up date is the maximum contact in between.
