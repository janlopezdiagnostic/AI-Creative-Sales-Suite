# Product plan: AI skills for non-tech trades

Status: prep / validation, October 2026. Owner has no business and no audience yet; this repo is the first asset. Direction set in the Oct 22 passive-income analysis: B2C / prosumer, under 3 hrs/week ongoing, test budget under $200 per idea, income continuing with minimal upkeep beats a quick spike.

## The gap

- Tech workers buy AI skills for coding, marketing, and writing. Marketplaces are full of them.
- Non-tech operators (trades, hospitality, events, classrooms) have the most repetitive text work per dollar earned: proposals, guest messages, timelines, review replies, parent emails. Almost no trade-specific AI playbooks exist for them, and the generic ones produce generic output they have to redo.
- They already buy templates (Etsy, Teachers Pay Teachers, Creative Market). A skill is the same purchase habit, upgraded: a template that rewrites itself for each job.

## Offer ladder

1. **Free (this repo):** lite skill per trade, one hero before/after example. Purpose: credibility, search traffic ("AI prompts for contractors" etc.), and proof for the 60-second demo videos.
2. **Pro Pack, $39-59 for the first three trades (Etsy + Gumroad; $19-69 across the full backlog):** full prompt library, guardrail libraries, PDF / Google Docs / Notion formats, visual setup guide, update rights. Purpose: the actual revenue product.
3. **Later, only if a trade proves out:** a bundle of all trades, and per-trade update subscriptions. No SaaS. No app. Files compound; software rots.

## Validation test (under $200, ~8 weeks total)

- Weeks 1-2: ship the 3 lite skills (done in this repo) and record one 60-second before/after video per trade.
- Week 3: post videos to TikTok / Reels and to 2-3 communities per trade (e.g. host, contractor, planner groups and subreddits). Link to this repo, with a waitlist / "Pro Pack coming" signup.
- Week 4: list the 3 Pro Packs on Etsy and Gumroad. Etsy listing fees are $0.20 per listing (plus any one-time shop setup fee); total planned spend is about $50, keeping $150 in reserve until a listing converts organically.
- Weeks 5-8 (the 30-day judging window): success signal per trade is 3+ Pro sales, or 50+ waitlist signups.

## Kill criteria

- A trade with no sale and fewer than 200 listing/repo views in 30 days after fixing titles and tags: drop it, do not build more prompts for it.
- Support above 2 hrs/week per $100 of revenue: the pack is under-documented; fix the setup guide once, then drop the trade if it persists.
- A platform ships the same job natively and free (e.g. Airbnb builds claim drafting into the host app): retire that skill and move the audience to the next trade.

## Risks, plainly

- Non-tech buyers do not browse GitHub. The repo is a credibility and search asset; sales happen on Etsy/Gumroad and through short video. The paid pack must ship as PDF + Google Docs, not just markdown.
- Contract, negotiation, and claim outputs carry legal-adjacent risk. Every skill carries a drafting-aid disclaimer and a review-before-sending rule.
- Platform policies (Airbnb, Vrbo) and trade rules change. Pro Pack updates are part of the price; budget 1-2 update passes per year per live trade, inside the 3 hrs/week cap.

## Unit snapshot (per Pro Pack sale, estimates)

- Price $39-59. Etsy take: $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + ~3% + $0.25 processing (an Etsy offsite-ads fee of 12-15% can also trigger on some sales). Gumroad take: ~10% + $0.50 plus processing; Gumroad Discover sales take a larger cut (~30%).
- At $49, net is roughly $41.90 (Gumroad) to $43.90 (Etsy) per sale before any ads. The planned test spend is about $50, so roughly 2 sales cover the spend; the validation bar is deliberately higher: 3+ sales per trade (9 across the three trades) inside the judging window.

> Repo note: this plan contains margins and kill criteria, so the repo starts **private**. If it ever flips public, move this file out first.
- No paid ads until a pack sells organically. Paid traffic for a sub-$200 test cannot generate enough conversions to judge (see the Oct 22 analysis: Meta quoted a $25/day delivery floor on an account with no history).
