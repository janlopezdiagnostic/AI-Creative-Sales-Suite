# Industry backlog

Scored on the same lens as the Oct 2026 passive-income analysis: pain in real money, willingness to pay, 60-second demo quality, and how passive the product stays once built. The first three are built as lite skills in this repo.

| Rank | Industry / skill | The job | Planned price | Why it ranks here | Biggest risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractors: Rough Notes to Bid | Voice notes to an itemized proposal with exclusions | $49 | Disputes cost real money; exclusions are trade judgment a generic chatbot misses | State-by-state wording; needs a not-legal-advice frame |
| 2 | Airbnb hosts: Message & Review Desk | Guest messages, review replies, claim drafts citing policy | $39 | Reviews and claims are direct revenue; hosts already pay for tools | Platform ships the feature natively; policy changes need updates |
| 3 | Wedding planners: Run-of-Show Builder | Intake to a buffered minute-by-minute timeline + vendor briefs | $59 | Buffer math is real expertise; demos beautifully | Small, template-habituated market (Etsy/Canva) |
| 4 | Realtors: Listing & Negotiation Kit | Listing copy with a fair-housing avoid-list; inspection-report triage into repair-request drafts | $39-69 | High transaction value makes $69 trivial | Crowded niche; brokerages hand out free tools; state legal nuance |
| 5 | Photographers: Client Experience Pack | Inquiry to booking to shot list to gallery delivery emails | $29-49 | Already buy workflow templates; family-formals sequencing is a real edge | HoneyBook / Dubsado feature creep |
| 6 | Restaurants: Menu Margin Desk | Invoice photo to plate costs, low-margin flags, menu rewrites | $49 | Margin math owners never do; photo-in/result-out demo | Owners are the hardest audience to reach online |
| 7 | Teachers: Differentiation Engine | One lesson to leveled versions + rubric + parent emails | $19-24 | Huge volume habit of buying (TpT) | Low price ceiling; free school tools (MagicSchool etc.) |
| 8 | Fitness coaches: Check-in Coach | Weekly check-ins to replies, tweaks, progress reports, red-flag scripts | $39 | Check-ins eat hours weekly | Health-adjacent liability; keep to communication, never advice |

## Explicitly not building

- Anything that gives financial, medical, or legal advice as its core job.
- Stock picks, "guaranteed" anything, or lead-gen arbitrage packs (killed in the Oct 2026 analysis for compliance and account-ban risk).
- Generic prompt packs ("100 ChatGPT prompts for business"). No trade judgment, no moat, race to free.
