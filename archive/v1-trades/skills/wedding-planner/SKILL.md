# Skill: Run-of-Show Builder (Wedding & Event Planners) - Lite

Turn a client intake into a minute-by-minute master timeline with realistic buffers, plus a short brief for each vendor. Lite version: core workflow only. The Pro Pack adds the full buffer tables by event type, rain-plan pivots, family-photo sequencing, and printable/Google Docs formats.

## Setup (once)

Fill in and keep with this skill:

```
Planner / business name:
Your standard buffers (e.g. hair & makeup per person, travel padding):
Photographers you work with and their shot-list habits:
Venues you know (load-in rules, curfews, kitchen timing):
Hard rules you never break (e.g. couple eats, sunset photos, doors-open buffer):
```

## Buffer rules the skill must apply (defaults, override in setup)

- Hair & makeup: ~45-60 min per person for makeup, ~30-45 for hair; never schedule the bride last with zero slack.
- Catering needs the room 90 min before doors; confirm against the venue, do not assume.
- Family photos: group combinations grow fast; sequence so elderly relatives are done first and released.
- Travel: venue-to-venue time from the intake, plus 50% padding in city traffic.
- Golden hour: take the sunset time from the intake (or confirm it with the planner before running); never guess it. Protect a 20-min couple-photo window inside it.
- Doors open: timeline lands the room ready 30 min before guests arrive, not at arrival.

## Rules

1. **Never invent a time input.** Sunset, travel, and vendor windows come from the intake or setup; missing = `[TO CONFIRM]` and a question list at the top.
2. **Every timeline block states who, where, and what they need in hand.**
3. **Conflicts are flagged, not smoothed over.** If the intake's wishes break the buffer math (e.g. 6 makeup clients, 2 artists, 9am start for a 10:30 ceremony), the output says so and offers the fix.
4. **One master timeline, many briefs.** Vendor briefs are slices of the same timeline, never separately invented schedules.

## Workflow

Input: completed intake (couple, venues, ceremony/reception times, party size, vendor list, must-have moments, family notes).
Output, in order:

1. **Questions / conflicts list**
2. **Master timeline** (minute-by-minute on the day, with buffers visible)
3. **Vendor briefs** (one short block each: photographer, caterer, DJ/band, florist, venue)
4. **Rain / delay pivot notes** for any outdoor block
