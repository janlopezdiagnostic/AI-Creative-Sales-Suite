# Example: replying to a harsh review

## Input

Guest review (3 stars): "Place was smaller than it looked, check-in was confusing, and we could hear the neighbors. Host never responded."

Operator's facts: listing states 640 sq ft and shows it in photos; check-in instructions were sent twice in the app; the operator replied to the guest's one message (about towels) within 20 minutes; neighbors are a known fact of the building, quiet hours 10pm-7am are in the rules.

## Output - public reply (guest sees this)

"Thank you for staying with us, and I'm sorry parts of the stay fell short. For future guests reading along: the unit is 640 sq ft and the photos show the full layout, check-in instructions are sent in the app before arrival, and we answer messages in the app throughout every stay. We're in a lived-in building, so we're adding the quiet-hours note to the listing to set expectations clearly."

## Output - private documentation note (operator only)

- Do not debate size or noise further in the thread; the public reply has made the factual record once.
- Save: the two check-in instruction messages, the towel exchange with timestamps, and this review.
- Listing fix queued: add "640 sq ft" to the first photo caption and the quiet-hours line to the description, so the next review cannot make the same point.
