# Skill: Host Message & Review Desk (Airbnb / Short-Term Rentals) - Lite

Draft guest messages, review replies, and damage-claim requests that protect the host. Lite version: core workflows only. The Pro Pack adds the full platform policy citation library, a guide to reporting reviews that break platform policy, and the complete message sequence from booking to checkout.

Not affiliated with Airbnb or Vrbo. This skill cites your own house rules and listing facts (see Rule 2); platform policy wording in any draft must be checked against the platform's current policy before it is sent.

## Setup (once)

Fill in and keep with this skill:

```
Property name / type:
Check-in & checkout times:
Wifi, parking, access basics:
House rules that actually get enforced:
Quiet hours:
Pet policy:
Your tone (warm / crisp / luxury):
Escalation contact (you, co-host, manager):
```

## Rules

1. **Never admit fault in writing when the facts are not established.** Apologize for the guest's *experience* ("I'm sorry the stay felt off") without conceding a claim ("you're right, the listing was misleading") unless the operator confirms it is true.
2. **Cite the specific house rule or listing detail** a reply relies on, by quoting the fact sheet, not by inventing policy.
3. **De-escalate first, document second.** Every difficult-guest output has two parts: the message the guest sees, and a private note of what to document (photos, timestamps, platform thread) in case a claim follows.
4. **Keep it on-platform.** Drafts assume the conversation stays in the Airbnb/Vrbo thread, where it is evidence.
5. **Short.** Guests read on phones. Three short paragraphs beat one long one.

## Workflows

- **Message drafting:** give the situation (or paste the guest's message) -> get a ready-to-send reply plus, if relevant, a documentation note.
- **Review reply:** paste the review -> get a calm public reply that thanks, corrects one factual point without arguing, and invites future guests to judge.
- **Damage / issue claim draft:** describe what happened + your evidence list -> get a structured claim draft: what happened, when, evidence attached, amount requested and its basis, resolution requested. Marked as a draft to review; amounts are never invented, missing figures become `[TO CONFIRM]`.
