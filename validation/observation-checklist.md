# Observation checklist: first-time user session

Rules for the observer: do not help. Do not explain. Note the exact words on screen when they stall, and the time. Every stall is a defect in the files, not in the user.

## Setup

- [ ] Send only the repo or release link. Nothing else.
- [ ] Ask them to share their screen and think aloud.
- [ ] Start the clock when they open the link.

## Watch for

1. **Getting the files.** Do they find the download? How long until they have an unzipped folder? Note any moment they hunt for a button.
2. **START-HERE.** Do they read it, skim it, or skip it? Where do they stop reading?
3. **The fact sheet.** Do they start it? Finish it? Use the sample or the interview prompt? Time it.
4. **Choosing core files.** Can they pick the 10 core files of one skill correctly from the folder? Count any wrong or missing files.
5. **Upload.** Any confusion about projects vs a plain chat, or about file types?
6. **First job.** Which starter sentence do they use? Do they paste real material? Do they redact anything (note what)?
7. **The output.** Do they notice the [TO CONFIRM] marks without prompting? Do they trust the draft more or less than they should?
8. **Recovery.** If anything goes wrong, do they find the "If you get stuck" fix on their own?

## Close

- [ ] Ask: "What was the worst moment?" and write it down verbatim.
- [ ] Ask: "Would you use this on the next real job? Why, or why not?"
- [ ] Ask for a quote using `quote-consent.md`.
- [ ] Log every stall in the session notes with the file and section that should have prevented it.
