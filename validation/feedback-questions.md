# Feedback questions (after one real job)

Ask these plainly, in this order, and record answers in the person's own words.

1. Which job did you use it for, and what did you give the assistant to work with?
2. What did the draft get right that a plain AI draft usually gets wrong?
3. What did it get wrong, miss, or invent?
4. Did you see any [TO CONFIRM] or [UNVERIFIED] marks? Did you know what to do with them?
5. What did you change before the draft went to the client?
6. How long did the whole thing take, and how long would it have taken without it?
7. What nearly stopped you from finishing the setup?
8. Would you use it again on the next job like this? What would have to change for a definite yes?
