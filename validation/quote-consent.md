# Quote consent

Ask this, in writing (email is fine), and keep the reply with the quote wherever it is used.

> "May I quote what you said about the Creative Sales Suite, with your first name and the kind of studio you run (for example, 'Maya, packaging studio')? I will show you the exact quote and where it will appear before anything is published, and you can withdraw it at any time."

Counts as a yes: an unambiguous written yes to that wording, after seeing the exact quote. Silence, a verbal maybe, or enthusiasm about the product are not consent. No quote is ever edited to change its meaning, and no result (revenue, win rate, time saved) is ever quoted as a number unless the person supplied it and agreed to its use.
