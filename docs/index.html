Your AI writes quotes like you sell software. You don't.

Ask a general AI tool to price a packaging job and you get a confident number with nothing underneath it: no revision rounds, no answer for when the client adds six more products, no usage rights, no source files. Then procurement asks for 30 percent off, and the only place that number can go is down.

The Creative Sales Suite is a set of instruction files you add to Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini. From then on, the assistant sells the way an experienced creative studio salesperson does: with your portfolio, your prices, and your terms. Built for founders, sales execs, and account execs at packaging, brand and product design, fashion, and agency teams. Made by someone who works in tech, for his wife (a designer) and friends in creative fields.

How it works

  1. Tell it about your studio, once. A one-page fact sheet: your work, your prices, your typical terms. About 15 minutes, with a filled-in sample to copy from. Or try it first with the sample studio, in 5 minutes, before filling in anything.
  2. Add the files to your AI. Upload the core files of the skill you need into a project or a chat. That is the whole setup.
  3. Ask for the job you need. "Research this brand." "Price this project." "They say we are too expensive, help me reply." You get a draft that follows the playbook and flags its own gaps, ready for your review.

Two rules built into every file

The assistant is instructed to never invent a fact, a result, a price, or a term. Anything missing is marked [TO CONFIRM] for you to decide, or [UNVERIFIED] when it could not be checked. And it never sends anything on its own. It drafts, you check, you send. It is also allowed to tell you no: no case study that fits means no outreach email, and a client with no real reason to buy gets a walk-away recommendation, not a pep talk.

Honest limits

The assistant follows these files carefully, and they are built to show their working and flag anything unchecked. It can still make mistakes, so you read every draft before a client sees it. If your AI cannot browse the web on your plan, research works from what you paste in, and the files instruct it to say so. Contract wording in the templates is a starting draft for your own adviser, not legal advice.
