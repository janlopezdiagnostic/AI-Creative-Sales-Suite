## The problem

Generic AI writes generic sales output. It does not know that your pitch is a product sample, that procurement will try to buy taste by the day, that the SKU count always grows, or that native source files are a priced deliverable, not a courtesy. So you spend your evenings fixing its drafts, or worse, sending them.

## What is inside

Eight skills covering the full sale, each a complete module: playbook, decision trees, worked examples from messy real inputs, templates, an edge-case library, and a self-check the AI runs before it answers.

1. **Account Research Brief:** buying signals with sources and dates, a fit score, and a pursue / watchlist / walk-away verdict. It is allowed to tell you not to pursue.
2. **Portfolio-Matched Outreach:** the right two or three case studies for that brand, and an opener that sounds like a peer, not a sequence.
3. **Discovery Call Kit:** prep, qualification, and a structured brief out of messy notes.
4. **Proposal & Deck Builder:** discovery notes to a proposal, without giving away spec creative.
5. **Scope and Pricing Architect:** tiered scopes with revision caps, usage and IP terms, kill fees, and change orders. It never invents a rate: your rate sheet or `[TO CONFIRM]`.
6. **Objection & Procurement Desk:** "too expensive," "in-house with AI," spec-work asks, RFP replies.
7. **Project-to-Retainer Converter:** the launch recap that becomes a retainer pitch, timed to your client's calendar.
8. **Revive & Referral:** stalled deals revived on real triggers, and intro asks at peak satisfaction.

Plus a vertical layer for your world. Packaging is first: retailer resets, regulatory deadlines, per-SKU economics, print production reality.

## It knows your tools

Figma first. Connect your assistant and the skills read file structure, components, tokens, and comments to count deliverables and judge complexity. No connection? Manual mode tells you exactly what to export instead. The same dual-mode pattern covers Notion, Google Workspace, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Apollo, Clay, PandaDoc, and Slack.

Two rules never bend: the AI reads first and writes only with your approval (nothing is ever sent silently), and it never invents facts, signals, rates, or terms.

## Honest limits

This is a files product. It does not click inside Figma by itself: connected mode needs your assistant's connector or MCP server, and every setup guide includes a 30-second test so you know which mode you are in. Contract language in the templates is a drafting aid for your counsel to review, not legal advice. And it will not fix a portfolio that does not match the client: Skill 1 will tell you that, plainly, before you waste a pitch.

## FAQ

**Which AI assistant do I need?** Any that can read files. Claude Projects is the smoothest default. Every skill also works by pasting files into a plain chat.

**Is my client data safe?** The files never send anything anywhere. What your assistant can see depends on what you paste or connect, and the setup guides walk through read-only defaults and NDA hygiene.

**Why not just use ChatGPT with good prompts?** That is the starting point this replaces. A prompt has no playbook, no decision trees, no edge-case library, no evals, and no memory of your rate sheet. These skills carry all of that, every session.
